2026-04-01   HR-POL-007 v2.4   Cl. 9.1   ** MATERIAL CHANGE **
    NOTICE PERIOD RESTRUCTURED BY BAND.
    Old position (HR-POL-007 v2.2, and HR-POL-001 v3.1 Cl. 16.1):
        90 calendar days for ALL confirmed employees.
    New position from 1 April 2026:
        Band B1 - B3 ......... 60 calendar days
        Band B4 and above .... 90 calendar days
        Probation, all bands . 30 calendar days
    Rationale: exit-interview and TA data showed the flat 90-day period was
    costing us candidates at B1-B3 without any corresponding delivery benefit;
    at B4+ the continuity risk is real and 90 days is retained.
    ACTION OUTSTANDING: the Employee Handbook (HR-POL-001) clause 16.1 still
    carries the flat 90-day figure. The Handbook is overdue for its scheduled
    review (was due 31 March 2026) and will be corrected in v4.0. Until then,
    HR-POL-007 v2.4 prevails - see HR-POL-001 Cl. 1.3, which provides that the
    detailed policy governs. The intranet HR FAQ page also still shows 90 days
    and has been raised with HR Communications (ticket NEX-HD-2026-04117).
    Approved: ELT, 3 March 2026.

2026-03-01   HR-POL-012 v1.4   Cl. 1   ** MATERIAL CHANGE **
    HYBRID OFFICE MINIMUM REDUCED FROM 12 TO 10 WORKING DAYS PER CALENDAR MONTH.
    Old position: minimum 12 working days per month in the assigned office.
    New position: minimum 10 working days per month, including the team anchor
    day in each week it falls.
    Rationale: January 2026 workplace survey (71% response rate). The 12-day
    minimum was being met by badge-in without corresponding co-location value;
    anchor-day attendance is the measure that correlates with team outcomes.
    ELT decision 11 February 2026.
    ACTION OUTSTANDING: HR-POL-001 v3.1 Cl. 4.6 and the intranet HR FAQ both
    still say 12 days. Both to be corrected - Handbook at v4.0, FAQ by HR
    Communications. Tracked under NEX-HD-2026-03042.

2026-01-01   HR-POL-004 v4.2   Cl. 6.2   ** MATERIAL CHANGE **
    COMPENSATORY OFF VALIDITY EXTENDED FROM 45 TO 60 DAYS.
    Old position: comp-off had to be availed within 45 days of being earned.
    New position: 60 days from the date earned, then it lapses. Still not
    encashable in any circumstance. Balance still capped at 6 at any time. Still
    not available at Band B6 and above.
    Rationale: 23% of comp-offs earned in FY 2024-25 lapsed unused, concentrated
    in Managed Services where incident work clusters at quarter end.
    ACTION OUTSTANDING: HR-POL-001 v3.1 Cl. 5.1 and the intranet HR FAQ both
    still say 45 days. Helpdesk agents have been briefed; two tickets in Q1
    FY26-27 were answered with the stale figure and have been corrected.

2026-01-01   HR-POL-004 v4.2   Cl. 10.2   ** NEW **
    Restrictions added on declining leave. A manager must record a specific
    business reason - "project pressure" is expressly not acceptable - and must
    offer an alternative window within 60 days. Leave may not be declined at all
    where it is certified sick leave, statutory parental or bereavement leave,
    a window declined twice before, or where declining would push the employee
    over the 30-day EL carry-forward cap.
