# HR Glossary

Terms used across Nexora HR policies, PeopleHub and payslips, in plain language. Where a
term has a precise policy definition, the governing document is named — that document
prevails over this page.

---

**Anchor day** — The one working day each week a team agrees to be in the office
together. Set by the team's manager and published a month ahead.
*See: HR-POL-012.*

 **Attendance regularisation** — Correcting a missed or wrong attendance record on
PeopleHub. Seven calendar days to raise it, three a month without HRBP approval.
*See: HR-POL-004 clause 8.4.*

 **Band** — Career level, B1 to B8. Drives approval authority, variable pay percentage,
notice period, travel class and relocation entitlement. It is not the same thing as a
designation: several designations can sit in one band.
*See: HR-POL-001 clause 2.4.*

**Basic Salary** — 40% of annual Fixed Pay at Nexora. Everything statutory is computed
from it: PF, gratuity, leave encashment, notice recovery, LOP.

**BGV** — Background verification. Run by VeriTrust Screening Services and initiated
about three weeks before joining.  Green,  Amber and Red outcomes; Red means the offer is
withdrawn or, post-joining, immediate termination.

**Buddy** — A colleague assigned to a new joiner for their first 30 days for practical
and social integration. Not a mentor, not a manager, and explicitly not the person who
handles performance, leave or grievances. INR 3,000 on completion.

**Carry forward** — Unused Earned Leave rolled into the next leave year, capped at 30
days. Anything above the cap is auto-encashed in the January payroll. Casual and Sick
Leave never carry forward.

**Casual Leave (CL)** — 12 days a year,  credited 3 days per quarter,  maximum 3
consecutive days at a time, lapses on 31 December, never encashable.

**Compa-ratio** — Your CTC divided by the midpoint of your band's salary range. Below
0.85 or above 1.15 needs written justification from the function head at every review.

**Compensatory Off (comp-off)** — Time off earned by working a weekly off or a holiday
at the manager's documented request. 4–8 hours earns half a day, 8+ hours earns a full
day. **Must be used within 60 days of being earned**, then it lapses. Never encashable.
Not available at Band B6 and above.
*See: HR-POL-004 clause 6. Note: the intranet HR FAQ still quotes the old 45-day window.*

**Confirmation** — The decision at the end of probation that makes you a confirmed
employee. Probation is six months. If no decision is recorded within 30 days of the
probation end date, you are deemed confirmed from that date.

**Core hours** — 11:00 to 17:00 IST. When meetings get scheduled and when you are
expected to be reachable, whether you are in the office or at home.

**CTC** — Cost to Company. Fixed Pay plus Variable Pay plus Retirals. It is not your
take-home pay and it is not your monthly salary × 12.

**DOJ** — Date of joining. The clock for leave accrual, gratuity, probation, long
service recognition and increment eligibility all start here.

**Earned Leave (EL)** — Also called Privilege Leave. 18 days a year, accrued 1.5 days per
completed month. The only leave type that carries forward and the only one encashable.

**EAP** — Employee Assistance Programme. Ashray Wellbeing Services, 1800-102-8899. Eight
free confidential counselling sessions per person per year, for you and each immediate
family member. Nexora sees anonymised numbers only.

**Encashment** — Converting unused Earned Leave to cash at (Basic + DA) ÷ 30 per day.
Happens automatically above the 30-day carry-forward cap each January, and on separation
for up to 30 days.

**F&F** — Full and final settlement. The closing account when you leave. Processed
within 45 days of the last working day.

**Fixed Pay** — The guaranteed annual cash part of CTC. Excludes variable pay and
retirals. Your monthly salary is Fixed Pay ÷ 12, before deductions.

**Flexi window** — Start any time between 08:00 and 11:00 IST, provided you complete
eight net hours and cover core hours.

**GMC / GPA / GTL** — Group Mediclaim (INR 5,00,000 family floater), Group Personal
Accident (3 × Fixed Pay, capped at INR 1,50,00,000), Group Term Life (3 × CTC). All
Company-paid. The parental top-up is the only one you pay for.

**Gratuity** — Payable after five years of continuous service: (15 ÷ 26) × last drawn
Basic × completed years. Waived on death or permanent disablement.

**HRBP** — HR Business Partner. The HR person assigned to your function. The escalation
point above your manager for anything people-related, and the person who attends
probation reviews,  grievance stage 2 and PIP opening and closing reviews.

**IC** — Internal Committee, constituted under the POSH Act at each site. Receives and
inquires into complaints of sexual harassment. Contact posh.ic@nexoratech.in.

**IntegriLine** — The independent whistleblowing hotline,  1800-419-6272, 24 hours, seven
languages, anonymous if you choose. Reports go to a third party and are summarised to
the Audit Committee, not to your management chain.

**Late mark** — Arrival after 11:00 IST without a prior exception. Three in a month costs
half a day of Casual Leave; more than eight is an attendance deficiency.

**Leave year** — 1 January to 31 December.  Not the same as the financial year or the
performance year, both of which run 1 April to 31 March. This catches people out every
 year.

**LOP** — Loss of Pay. A day of absence with no leave balance to cover it. Deducted at
monthly gross ÷ calendar days, and it reduces PF for that month.

**LWD** — Last working day. The end of the notice period, extended by any leave taken
during notice unless the function head waives it.

**Normalisation Council** — The forum that checks a rating means the same thing whoever
gave it. Function head, all B5+ managers in the function, HRBP facilitating.

**Notice period** — 60 calendar days at Band B1–B3, 90 calendar days at Band B4 and

above, 30 days during probation.
*See: HR-POL-007 clause 9.1. Note: the Employee Handbook v3.1 and the intranet HR FAQ
still say 90 days for everyone; the Compensation policy of 1 April 2026 supersedes them.*

**Notice shortfall recovery** — What you pay if an approved short notice is agreed:
(Basic + DA) ÷ 30 per day of shortfall.

**On-call allowance** — INR 700 per weekday and INR 1,200 per weekend or holiday day of
roster, plus INR 1,500 per callout resolved outside working hours, capped at two per day.

**PeopleHub** — The HR system of record. Leave, attendance, payslips, goals, policies,
nominations, helpdesk. If it is not on PeopleHub, for HR purposes it did not happen.

**PIP** — Performance Improvement Plan. 60 days, extendable once by 30. Follows a rating
of 1 or a material decline.

**POSH** — Prevention of Sexual Harassment, under the 2013 Act. Nexora extends the same
mechanism and protections to employees of every gender as a matter of policy.

**Probation** — Six months for every new hire, lateral or fresher. Extendable once by up
to three months. Notice during probation is 30 days on either side.

**Retirals** — The part of CTC that is not cash to you now: employer PF, the gratuity
provision at 4.81% of Basic, and employer NPS if you elected it.

 **Sabbatical** — Up to six months unpaid, after five years of service, with a 12-month
service commitment on return. Gratuity service continues to count; leave accrual and PF
do not.

**Sick Leave (SL)** — 12 days a year, all credited on 1 January. Medical certificate
 required above two consecutive days. Up to five days a year may be used for the
hospitalisation of a spouse, dependent child or dependent parent.

**Variable Pay** — The performance-linked part of CTC: 5% at B1–B2 rising to 20% at
B7–B8. Paid annually with the June payroll. Not payable if you have resigned or are
serving notice on the disbursement date.

**Weekly off** — Saturday and Sunday, unless the engagement's Delivery Operating Model
 records a different pattern.

**WFH** — The attendance code for an approved remote working day.  It is a place of work,
not a leave type, and it does not touch any leave balance.
