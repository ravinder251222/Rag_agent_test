# 30-60-90 Day Plan

Every new joiner at Nexora gets one. The manager drafts it before the joining date, the
two of you agree it by the end of week one, and it is reviewed at day 30, day 60 and day
90. It lives on PeopleHub → Onboarding → 30-60-90. It is not a formality: the day 90
review is the first formal probation gate and this document is what it is assessed
against.

## Why the three horizons

| Horizon | The question it answers | Failure mode it prevents |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30: Learn | Do you understand the system, the client and the team? | Shipping confident changes to a system you have misread |
| Days 31–60: Contribute | Can you do the work with normal supervision? | Staying in observation mode for a quarter |
| Days 61–90: Own | Can you be relied on for an outcome? | Being technically productive but not accountable for anything |

## How to write one that is worth reading

1. **Six to ten items in total, not twenty.** A plan nobody can hold in their head is a
   plan nobody reviews.
2. **Every item has an observable outcome.** "Understand the architecture" is not an
   outcome.  "Present a written summary of the settlement flow at Thursday's stand-up"
   is.
3. **Every item has a date, not a horizon.** "Day 25", not "in the first month".
4. **Name the people.** "Meet ten people" becomes real when the ten are named.
5. **One item should be uncomfortable.** A new joiner who completes a plan without ever
   being stretched has been under-scoped.
6. **The manager owns half of it.** Access, introductions, a first task that is real but
   safe, and time in the diary — those are manager commitments and belong in the plan
   explicitly.

## Template

```
DAYS 1-30 — LEARN
[ ] Complete all seven mandatory compliance courses            by day 5
[ ] Environment set up; full test suite green locally          by day 7
[ ] <client/product> architecture read; written summary shared by day __
[ ] First small change merged end to end                       by day __
[ ] Meet: <name>, <name>, <name>, <name>, <name>               by day 30
[ ] Day 30 review with manager                                    day 30


DAYS 31-60 — CONTRIBUTE
[ ] Own <named module / service / test suite / workstream>     by day __
[ ] Deliver <named piece of work> end to end                   by day __
[ ] Lead estimation for at least two stories                   by day __
[ ] First independent client-facing interaction                by day __
[ ] Day 60 review; buddy programme closes                         day 60

DAYS 61-90 — OWN
[ ] Deliver <agreed outcome> independently and on schedule     by day __
[ ] Improve one thing nobody asked you to improve              by day __

[ ] Join <on-call roster / review rota / interview panel>      by day __
[ ] Onboard or buddy the next joiner                           by day __
[ ] Agree development goals for the rest of the year              day 90
```

## Worked example — Senior Software Engineer (B3),  BFSI,  Gurugram

### Days 1–30

- Day 5 — all seven mandatory courses complete on NexoraLearn.
- Day 7 — local environment running; full test suite green; first pipeline run observed.
- Day 12 — client environment access granted; client payments-data handling module done.
- Day 15 — read the Settlement Services solution architecture document; present a
  one-page summary at Thursday stand-up.
- Day 25 — three "Low" complexity defects picked up and merged.
- Day 30 — met the Delivery Manager,  the client technical lead,  the QE lead, the
  architect and four team members. Notes recorded.

### Days 31–60

- Day 40 — took ownership of the Reconciliation Engine module and its on-call runbook.
- Day 45 — led estimation for two stories in sprint planning.
- Day 50 — ran the first client status call without the buddy in the room.
- Day 55 — ISO 20022 message mapping change delivered end to end: design note,
  implementation, unit and integration tests, review, UAT deployment.
- Day 60 — flaky test count in the module down from eleven to zero.

### Days 61–90

- Day 70 — joined the on-call roster after shadowing two rotations.
- Day 80 — CI pipeline improvement proposed, implemented and measured (build time down
  from 14 minutes to 9).
- Day 85 — Q3 statement-generation enhancement delivered to production.

- Day 90 — buddying the next joiner to the team; development goals agreed for the rest
  of the year.

## Worked example — HR Business Partner (B3),  Bengaluru


### Days 1–30

- Day 5 — mandatory courses complete.
- Day 10 — PeopleHub HRBP modules walked through with HR Operations; reporting access
  confirmed.
- Day 14 — read the last two quarters of attrition, absence and grievance data for the
  supported function; write down the three things that surprise you.
- Day 20 — attend one full performance calibration session as an observer.
- Day 30 — one-to-ones held with all eight managers in the supported function.

### Days 31–60

- Day 40 — own the monthly attendance exception review for the function end to end.

- Day 48 — run the first employee relations conversation with the HR Manager observing.
- Day 55 — deliver the POSH refresher session to one team.
- Day 60 — draft the function's workforce plan for the next two quarters.

### Days 61–90

- Day 75 — handle a grievance at Stage 2 independently, with documented outcome.
- Day 82 — propose one change to how the function runs probation reviews, with evidence.
- Day 90 — own the function's goal-setting cycle communications.

## Review gates

| Gate | Who is in the room | What is decided |
|---|---|---|
| Day 30 | New joiner, manager | On track or needs support; is training complete |
| Day 60 | New joiner, manager, buddy | On track or needs support; buddy programme closes |
| Day 90 | New joiner, manager, HRBP | Formal probation review 1 |
| Day 180 | New joiner, manager, HRBP | Probation review 2 and the confirmation decision |

A "needs support" outcome at day 30 or day 60 is not a bad outcome — it is the system
working. What is not acceptable is arriving at day 90 with a surprise. If a manager has
concerns at day 40, the new joiner should hear them at day 40.
