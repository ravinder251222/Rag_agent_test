# Remote and Hybrid Work Guidelines

Document ID: HR-POL-012. Effective 1 March 2026. Supersedes v1.2 dated 1 April 2025.

## 1. Designations

Every role carries one of three workplace designations, recorded on PeopleHub against
the position, not against the person.

| Designation | Who | Office expectation |
|---|---|---|
| **Site-Anchored** | IT Support, Facilities, Reception, Security, lab and hardware engineering, and any role on a client engagement with a contractual on-site clause | Every working day |
| **Hybrid** | The default for every other role | **A minimum of 10 working days per calendar month**,  including the team's anchor day each week it falls |
| **Fully Remote** | Exception only | None, subject to quarterly on-site visits |

> **Change in v1.4.** The hybrid minimum was reduced from twelve working days per month
> to **ten**, effective 1 March 2026, following the January 2026 workplace survey and the
> ELT decision of 11 February 2026.  Older material — including the Employee Handbook
> v3.1 and the HR FAQ page on the intranet — still quotes twelve. Ten is correct. The
> Handbook will be corrected at its next revision.

## 2. Anchor days

Each team sets one anchor day per week, published by the manager on the team calendar by
the last working day of the preceding month. The purpose of an anchor day is co-location
for the work that is genuinely better in a room: design discussion, planning,
retrospectives, onboarding, difficult conversations, and eating lunch with people you
work with.

 Anchor days are not attendance theatre. A manager who sets an anchor day and then
schedules the whole day as back-to-back video calls has wasted everyone's commute.

## 3. Fully Remote status

Fully Remote is an exception, not a tier. It requires:

- twelve months of continuous service;
- a role with no on-site dependency, confirmed by the Delivery Manager where the role is
  client-facing;
- the written approval of the function head and the CHRO;
- a workspace declaration (see section 6); and
- review every six months, at which the status may be withdrawn with 30 days' notice.

Fully Remote colleagues attend their assigned office at least once per quarter, at
Company cost, and are expected at the annual team offsite.

## 4.  Where you may work from

| Situation | Position |
|---|---|
| Your registered home address | Permitted for all Hybrid and Fully Remote colleagues |
| Another address within India, up to 30 days | Permitted; inform your manager and update PeopleHub |
| Another address within India,  beyond 30 days | Needs HRBP approval; may change your professional tax and payroll state |
| Another Nexora office | Permitted; book a desk on the Facilities tile |
| A co-working space | Permitted only from the approved list; never for client calls involving confidential material |
| Outside India | **Not permitted** without the prior written approval of the CHRO and Legal — it creates tax residency, permanent establishment and data transfer exposure |
| A café, airport lounge, hotel lobby or any public space | Permitted for reading and writing; never for client calls, screen-sharing or anything classified Confidential or Restricted |

## 5.  Working hours when remote

The rules do not change because you are at home. Core hours remain 11:00 to 17:00 IST.
The flexible start window remains 08:00 to 11:00.  You still record attendance — the
PeopleHub check-in through the VPN client covers a remote day, and the day appears as
`WFH` in the leave and attendance ledger.

What does change is the standard of communication expected. When your colleagues cannot
see you, you have to say more:

- Update your Slack status when you step away for more than 30 minutes.
- Post a short written update in the team channel on every remote day. Two lines is
  enough.
- Answer within the working day,  or say when you will.
- Keep your calendar accurate. An empty calendar is read as "available", and that is
  usually not what people mean.

## 6. Equipment, connectivity and workspace

| Item | Provision |
|---|---|
| Laptop and peripherals | Standard kit per HR-POL-001 clause 11.1; the monitor and dock may be taken home with manager approval recorded on NexServe |
| Internet and telephone allowance | INR 1,500 per month, paid with salary against a declaration, no bills required |
| Minimum connection | 20 Mbps down, 5 Mbps up, with a fallback (mobile hotspot is acceptable) |
| Power continuity | A UPS or inverter for the router is claimable once every three years, up to INR 8,000 |
| Chair and desk | Not provided; an ergonomic assessment is available on request through the HRBP, and equipment supported by an occupational-health note is provided |
| Workspace declaration | A short PeopleHub form confirming a workable, reasonably private setup — required for Fully Remote, optional for Hybrid |

## 7. Security when working away from the office

1. VPN on, always, before you touch anything work-related.
2. Screen lock when you step away, including at home. Household members are not
   authorised users.
3. Headphones for every call where client material is discussed.
4. No printing of Confidential or Restricted material outside the office.
5. No personal removable storage on the Nexora device, ever.
6.  No family use of the Company laptop,  not even to print a school form.
7. Video-call backgrounds must not reveal whiteboards, screens, documents or anything
   else you would not hand to a stranger.

## 8. Manager responsibilities

- Publish the anchor day a month ahead.
- Do not treat presence as a proxy for contribution, and do not treat absence as a
  proxy for its lack.
- Run meetings so that a remote participant is a full participant: one person one

  camera, notes in writing, decisions recorded in the channel and not just in the room.
 - Make sure the person who is remote today is not the person who finds out last.
- Check in on the colleague who has not been to the office in six weeks. Not to police
  them — because isolation is the most common thing hybrid working gets wrong.

## 9. When this does not work

Hybrid status may be varied or withdrawn where there is a documented performance
concern, a client contractual requirement, a security incident linked to remote working,
or a persistent failure to meet the monthly office minimum. Withdrawal is a managed
conversation with the HRBP present and 30 days' notice, not an instruction in a message.
