## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose

This Policy sets out how Nexora Technologies Private Limited determines, structures,
administers and pays compensation and benefits. It exists so that pay decisions are
consistent, explainable, compliant and defensible, and so that every employee can understand
how their own pay is constructed and when it will reach their account.

### 1.2 Scope

This Policy applies to all employees on the Nexora payroll at Gurugram, Bengaluru and Pune,
including probationers, Graduate Engineer Trainees and fixed-term contract employees, except
where a clause states otherwise. It does not apply to independent contractors, consultants
engaged on a statement of work, or staffing-partner deputees, whose remuneration is governed
by the relevant contract.

### 1.3 Compensation Philosophy

Nexora's compensation philosophy rests on five commitments:

1. **Market-informed.** Fixed pay is benchmarked annually against the Indian IT services
   market using the Aon and Mercer India Technology surveys, with Nexora targeting the
   **60th percentile** of the peer set for Band B1–B4 and the **50th percentile** for Band B5
   and above, where the mix shifts towards variable pay.
2. **Internally equitable.** Two employees in the same band, role family, location and
   performance tier should be paid within a defensible range of each other. Compa-ratio
   dispersion is reviewed annually.
3. **Performance-differentiated.** Increment and variable pay outcomes differ materially by
   rating. A rating of 5 must produce a visibly different outcome from a rating of 3.
4. **Transparent in structure.** Every employee can see how their CTC is constructed, what
   each component means, and how each is taxed.
5. **Compliant without exception.** Statutory obligations under the Employees' Provident
   Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952, the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, the
   Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, the Income-tax Act, 1961, the
   Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948 and applicable State Professional Tax legislation are
   met in full and on time.

### 1.4 Pay Equity

Nexora conducts an annual pay equity review, completed before the increment cycle closes,
comparing median fixed pay by gender within each band, role family and location cohort of ten
or more. Any unexplained gap above 3% is corrected in the same cycle through a targeted
adjustment budget held by the CHRO, outside the function's increment pool. The result of the
review is reported to the Board's Nomination and Remuneration Committee each year.

## 2. Compensation Structure

### 2.1 The Three Elements of CTC

Cost to Company (CTC) is the aggregate annual cost of employing a person. It comprises:

| Element | Description | Guaranteed? |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed Pay | Monthly cash salary and annual allowances | Yes |
| Variable Pay | Performance-linked annual or quarterly payout | No |
| Retirals | Employer PF, gratuity provision, employer NPS where elected | Yes, subject to statute |

CTC is **not** take-home pay. Take-home pay is Fixed Pay less employee statutory deductions
(PF, professional tax, income tax) and less any voluntary deductions.

### 2.2 Fixed Pay Construction

| Component | Basis | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Basic Salary | 40% of annual Fixed Pay | Drives PF, gratuity, leave encashment, notice recovery |
| House Rent Allowance (HRA) | 50% of Basic Salary | Exemption under Section 10(13A) claimed on rent proof |
| Leave Travel Allowance (LTA) | INR 40,000 per annum | Exempt under Section 10(5) for two journeys in a block of four calendar years |
| Meal Card (NexoCard) | INR 2,200 per month | Taxed as a perquisite in accordance with prevailing rules |
| Telephone and Internet Allowance | INR 1,500 per month | Payable to Hybrid and Fully Remote employees against a declaration |
| Books and Periodicals Allowance | INR 1,000 per month | Reimbursed against bills |
| Special Allowance | Balancing figure | Fully taxable |

The current block for LTA purposes is the calendar years 2026 to 2029.

Employees who have elected the new tax regime under Section 115BAC may opt for a simplified
structure comprising Basic Salary at 50% of Fixed Pay and Special Allowance as the balancing
figure, since most exemptions are unavailable under that regime. The election is made once a
year in April on PeopleHub → My Pay → Structure Election.

| Stage | Window | Owner |

| Effective date | 1 July | Payroll |

### 3.2 Increment Guidelines for the Cycle Effective 1 July 2026

The overall increment budget approved by the Board for FY 2026-27 is **8.4% of the aggregate
fixed-pay base**. Indicative individual outcomes are:

| Rating | Label | Indicative increment range | Variable pay multiplier | Promotion eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding | 14% – 18% | 1.50x | Eligible |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | 10% – 13% | 1.20x | Eligible |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | 6% – 9% | 1.00x | Eligible on second consecutive 3 with readiness assessment |
| 2 | Partially Meets Expectations | 0% – 4% | 0.50x | Not eligible |
| 1 | Below Expectations | Nil | Nil | Not eligible; PIP applies |

Additional adjustments outside the guideline may be applied for:

(a) a compa-ratio below 0.85 where performance is 3 or above (market correction);
(b) a critical-skill premium approved by the practice head and the CHRO;
(c) a pay equity correction under Clause 1.4; and
(d) a promotion increment under Clause 3.3.

### 3.3 Promotion Increment

A promotion carries an increment in addition to the merit increment, calculated so that the
new CTC is not below the minimum of the new band:

| Band movement | Promotion increment (over and above merit) |
|---|---|
| B1 → B2, B2 → B3 | 8% – 12% |
| B3 → B4 | 10% – 14% |
| B4 → B5 | 12% – 16% |
| B5 → B6 and above | 12% – 18% |

Promotions are effective 1 July, in the same cycle as the compensation review. Off-cycle
promotions require the approval of the CHRO and are limited to 1% of function headcount in a
financial year.

### 3.4 Eligibility

An employee is eligible for the annual increment if they:

(a) joined on or before **31 December** of the preceding year — those joining between
    1 January and 31 March receive a pro-rated increment at 50% of the guideline; those
    joining on or after 1 April are not eligible in the immediately following cycle;
(b) are confirmed, or are due for confirmation before 1 July;
(c) hold a rating of 2 or above; and
(d) have not resigned and are not serving notice on the date the letters are released.

### 3.5 Off-cycle Revisions

Off-cycle compensation revisions are made only for a counter-offer retention case approved by
the CHRO and the function head, a role change involving a band movement, a market correction
identified in the equity review, or the correction of an administrative error. Counter-offer
retention is used sparingly; a function may not exceed four counter-offers in a financial
year without ELT approval.

## 4. Variable Pay

### 4.1 Structure

Variable pay opportunity, expressed as a percentage of CTC, is:

| Band | Variable pay as % of CTC | Payout frequency |
|---|---|---|
| B1 – B2 | 5% | Annual, with June payroll |
| B3 | 8% | Annual, with June payroll |
| B4 | 12% | Annual, with June payroll |
| B5 – B6 | 15% | Annual, with June payroll |
| B7 – B8 | 20% | Annual, with June payroll |
| Sales and Account Management (all bands) | 25% – 40% | Quarterly, per the Sales Incentive Plan (FIN-POL-014) |

### 4.2 Calculation

> Variable payout = Variable pay opportunity × Company multiplier × Business unit multiplier
> × Individual multiplier × Eligibility fraction

- **Company multiplier** (0.6 to 1.3) is determined by the Board against the annual revenue
  and EBITDA plan.
- **Business unit multiplier** (0.7 to 1.3) is determined by the ELT against the business
  unit's revenue, margin, utilisation and client satisfaction targets.
- **Individual multiplier** is the rating multiplier from Clause 3.2.
- **Eligibility fraction** is the proportion of the assessment year (1 April to 31 March) for
  which the employee was on the payroll and not on unpaid leave, expressed to two decimals.

Worked example: a Band B4 employee on CTC INR 30,00,000, rated 4, who joined on 1 October
2025, in a year where the Company multiplier is 0.95 and the business unit multiplier is 1.10.

> Opportunity = 12% × 30,00,000 = INR 3,60,000
> Payout = 3,60,000 × 0.95 × 1.10 × 1.20 × 0.50 = **INR 2,25,720**

### 4.3 Eligibility and Exclusions

An employee is not eligible for variable pay where they have resigned, are serving notice, or
have been served notice of termination on the date of disbursement; where they hold a rating
of 1; where they are on a live final written warning under HR-POL-001, Clause 15.2; or where
they are on a Performance Improvement Plan that has not been successfully closed.

Employees on Maternity, Paternity or Adoption Leave during the assessment year are treated as
being on the payroll for the full period of that leave, and the leave does not reduce the
eligibility fraction.

Variable pay is discretionary. Payment in one year creates no entitlement in any subsequent
year.

## 5. Payroll Administration

### 5.1 The Payroll Calendar

| Event | Day |
|---|---|
| Payroll month | 1st to the last day of the calendar month |
| Attendance, leave and input cut-off | 18th of the month |
| Investment declaration and proof window | As per Clause 7.3 |
| Payroll processing and internal review | 19th to 24th |
| Bank file released | 25th to 27th |
| Salary credited | **Last working day of the month** |
| Payslip published on PeopleHub → My Pay | By the 3rd working day of the following month |
| Statutory remittances (PF, PT, TDS) | By the 15th, 20th and 7th of the following month respectively |

Where the last working day falls on a bank holiday, salary credit is advanced to the
preceding working day. Any input received after the 18th — a leave regularisation, a shift
certification, a reimbursement approval, a bank-detail change — is processed in the following
month's payroll and no off-cycle payment is made except under Clause 5.4.

### 5.2 Payslips and Statements

Payslips are published as password-protected PDFs on PeopleHub → My Pay. The password is the
employee's PeopleHub ID in lower case followed by the date of birth in DDMMYYYY format. The
payslip shows earnings by component, deductions, the leave balance snapshot as at the last
day of the month, and the year-to-date tax computation.

The following statements are also available on PeopleHub:

| Statement | Availability |
|---|---|
| Monthly payslip | 3rd working day of the following month |
| Annual tax computation sheet | Live, refreshed nightly |
| Form 16 (Parts A and B) | By 15 June for the preceding financial year |
| Form 12BA (perquisites) | With Form 16 |
| Provident Fund passbook link | Live, via the EPFO member portal |
| Full and final settlement statement | With the settlement payment |
| Income and employment verification letter | On request, 2 working days |

### 5.3 Bank Account and Payment

Salary is paid by direct credit to a single bank account in the employee's own name. Joint
accounts are permitted only where the employee is the primary holder. Salary is not paid to a
third-party account under any circumstances.

Bank account changes are made on PeopleHub → My Profile → Bank Details, require an uploaded
cancelled cheque or a bank statement header showing the name, account number and IFSC, and
are subject to a verification call from the Payroll team. Changes received after the 18th take
effect the following month.

### 5.4 Off-cycle Payments

Off-cycle payments are made only for: a salary payment missed through a Company error; a full
and final settlement; a statutory payment such as gratuity or maternity benefit; and an
emergency medical advance under HR-POL-001, Clause 7.5. All off-cycle payments require the
joint approval of the Head of Payroll and the CFO.

### 5.5 Overpayment and Recovery

Where an overpayment is identified, Payroll notifies the employee in writing with a
computation. Recovery is made from subsequent salary in instalments not exceeding 20% of net
monthly salary, or in a single deduction where the employee consents in writing, or from the
full and final settlement. Recovery from a separating employee that exceeds the settlement
amount is pursued as a debt.

### 6.1 Provident Fund

| Item | Treatment |

| Employee contribution | 12% of Basic Salary |
| Employer contribution | 12% of Basic Salary, restricted to the statutory wage ceiling of INR 15,000 per month (INR 1,800) unless the employee has elected contribution on full Basic |
| Split of employer contribution | 8.33% to the Employees' Pension Scheme, subject to the ceiling; balance to the Provident Fund |
| Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF) | Up to 88% of Basic; election accepted in April and October only |

| UAN | Allotted or linked at joining; visible on PeopleHub |
| Withdrawal and transfer | Through the EPFO Unified Member Portal; Payroll assists with the employer attestation |

Employees whose Basic Salary at the time of first joining the Provident Fund exceeded
INR 15,000 per month and who were not previously members may elect to be excluded, by
submitting Form 11 at joining. The election is irrevocable for the duration of employment
with Nexora.

### 6.2 Gratuity

Payable under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972 on separation after five (5) years of
continuous service. The five-year condition does not apply on death or permanent disablement.

> Gratuity = (15 ÷ 26) × last drawn (Basic + Dearness Allowance) × completed years of service

A period of more than six months in the final year counts as a full year. The statutory
exemption limit under Section 10(10) of the Income-tax Act, 1961 applies; amounts above the
limit are taxable. Gratuity is paid within thirty (30) days of it becoming payable. Nexora
maintains a funded gratuity trust with Sarvodaya Life Assurance.

Nomination is made in Form F at joining and must be updated within thirty (30) days of
marriage or of a change in family circumstances.

### 6.3 Employees' State Insurance

ESI applies to employees whose monthly gross wages do not exceed INR 21,000. The employee
contributes 0.75% and the employer 3.25% of gross wages. At Nexora this applies principally
to a small number of Band B1 support roles and to interns receiving a stipend above the
threshold. Covered employees are issued an ESI number and a Pehchan card and are directed to
the designated ESI dispensary for their residence.

### 6.5 Labour Welfare Fund

Contributions are made as required in Karnataka and Maharashtra at the notified rates and
frequencies. The employee's share is deducted in the December payroll for Karnataka and in
the June and December payrolls for Maharashtra.

### 6.6 Statutory Bonus

Employees whose Basic Salary plus Dearness Allowance does not exceed INR 21,000 per month are
paid a statutory bonus under the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965, at the rate declared for the
financial year, subject to the statutory minimum of 8.33% and maximum of 20% of the salary
computed on a notional ceiling of INR 7,000 per month or the applicable minimum wage,
whichever is higher. The bonus is paid in the November payroll each year.

## 7. Income Tax and Declarations

### 7.1 Tax Regimes

Employees may elect between the old tax regime, with exemptions and deductions, and the new
tax regime under Section 115BAC, with lower slab rates and limited deductions. The default
regime is the new regime; an employee wishing to remain on the old regime must make a
positive election.

The election is made on PeopleHub → My Pay → Tax Regime in April each year and is applied to
the withholding for that financial year. The regime elected for withholding does not bind the
employee at the time of filing the return of income.

### 7.2 Investment Declaration

| Stage | Window | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Provisional declaration | 1 – 25 April | Basis for withholding from the April payroll |
| Mid-year revision | 1 – 20 October | Adjusts withholding for the balance of the year |
| Proof submission | 1 – 31 January | Declarations without proof are withdrawn |
| Final adjustment | February and March payrolls | Shortfall or excess recovered or adjusted |

An employee who does not submit a provisional declaration by 25 April has tax withheld on the
gross salary without any exemption or deduction, adjusted retrospectively once the declaration
is received.

### 7.3 Common Proofs Accepted

| Deduction or exemption | Proof required |
|---|---|
| HRA (Section 10(13A)) | Rent receipts; rent agreement; landlord PAN where annual rent exceeds INR 1,00,000 |
| Section 80C | LIC premium receipts, PPF passbook, ELSS statement, tuition fee receipts, principal repayment certificate |
| Section 80D | Premium receipt for parental top-up or an independent policy; preventive health check-up bill |
| Section 24(b) | Provisional interest certificate from the lender; possession certificate |
| Section 80E | Education loan interest certificate |
| LTA (Section 10(5)) | Boarding pass, ticket, invoice for the journey within India |
| Section 80CCD(1B) | NPS Tier-I contribution statement |
| Previous employer income | Form 12B with the previous employer's Form 16 or tax computation |

New joiners must submit Form 12B within thirty (30) days of joining. Where it is not
submitted, Nexora withholds tax on Nexora income alone, which frequently results in a
shortfall at the time of filing; the responsibility for the shortfall rests with the employee.

### 7.4 Perquisites

Perquisites valued and reported in Form 12BA include the meal card credit above the exempt
limit, the employer's contribution to the Provident Fund, the National Pension System and any
superannuation fund in excess of INR 7,50,000 in aggregate in a financial year together with
the annual accretion thereon, an interest-free or concessional loan above INR 20,000, and any
Company-provided accommodation.

## 9. Separation Payments

### 9.1 Notice Period and Notice Pay

The notice period applicable to a confirmed employee, on resignation or on termination other
than for cause, is:

| Band | Notice period |
|---|---|
| B1 – B3 | 60 calendar days |
| B4 and above | 90 calendar days |
| During probation, all bands | 30 calendar days |

Where a shortfall in notice is approved by the function head and the CHRO, recovery is made
at:

> Notice shortfall recovery per day = (Monthly Basic Salary + Dearness Allowance) ÷ 30

Notice pay recovered is not a payment for services and does not attract Provident Fund. Where
the Company waives notice, no recovery is made and the last working day is advanced.

### 9.2 Full and Final Settlement

The settlement is processed within **forty-five (45) days** of the last working day and is
credited to the employee's registered bank account. It comprises:

**Payable to the employee**

| Item | Basis |
|---|---|
| Salary for days worked in the final month | Gross ÷ calendar days × days worked |
| Earned Leave encashment | Up to 30 days at (Basic + DA) ÷ 30 |
| Gratuity, where payable | Clause 6.2 |
| Statutory bonus, where applicable, on a pro-rata basis | Clause 6.6 |
| Variable pay declared before the resignation date and not yet paid | Clause 4.3 |
| Approved and unpaid reimbursements | NexSpend records |
| Shift, on-call and callout allowances certified but unpaid | Roster records |

**Recoverable from the employee**

| Item | Basis |
|---|---|
| Notice shortfall | Clause 9.1 |
| Outstanding salary advance or medical advance | Balance outstanding |
| Relocation assistance | 100% if separating within 12 months of relocation; 50% between 12 and 24 months |
| Joining bonus | 100% if separating within 12 months |
| Retention bonus | As per the retention letter |
| Higher education assistance | Pro rata over the 24-month bond |
| Certification reimbursement above INR 1,00,000 in aggregate | Pro rata over the 12-month commitment |
| Unreturned assets | Depreciated value, capped at INR 40,000 per asset |
| Excess leave availed | (Basic + DA) ÷ 30 per day |
| Loss of Pay days | Gross ÷ calendar days per day |

Where the recoverable amount exceeds the payable amount, the difference is a debt due to the
Company and is demanded in writing. The relieving letter is issued irrespective of the
settlement outcome, provided the employee has completed asset return and clearance, and is
not withheld as a means of recovering money.

### 9.3 Form 16 and Post-separation Documents

Form 16 for the year of separation is issued by 15 June following the end of that financial
year and is emailed to the employee's personal email address on record. Employees must ensure
that the personal email address on PeopleHub is current before the last working day. Requests
for a duplicate Form 16 or an employment verification letter after separation are made to
alumni.support@nexoratech.in and are actioned within five (5) working days.

### 10.2 Confidentiality of Compensation Information

An employee's compensation is confidential between the employee, their reporting manager,
the function head, the HRBP, the Compensation and Benefits team and Payroll. Employees are
free to discuss their own compensation with anyone; nothing in this Policy restricts that.
What is prohibited is the disclosure of **another** employee's compensation by a person who
obtained it in the course of their duties, which is a breach of HR-POL-002, Clause 3.1.

Managers must not disclose one team member's outcome to another, and must not use a
comparison to another named individual when communicating an outcome.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. My CTC is INR 24,00,000. Why is my monthly credit not INR 2,00,000?**
Because CTC includes variable pay and retirals, which are not part of the monthly cash
salary, and because statutory and tax deductions are made from the fixed monthly amount. See
the worked example at Clause 2.3.

**Q2. When will I get my increment letter?**
Letters are released on 25 June and the revision is effective 1 July, appearing first in the
July payroll credited on the last working day of July.

**Q3. I joined in February 2026. Am I eligible for the July 2026 increment?**
No. Employees joining on or after 1 April of the assessment year are not eligible in the
immediately following cycle. Employees joining between 1 January and 31 March receive 50% of
the guideline.

**Q4. I resigned on 10 May. Will I receive variable pay in June?**
No. Employees who have resigned or are serving notice on the date of disbursement are not
eligible.

**Q5. Can I add my parents to the medical policy in September?**
Only if you joined in the preceding thirty days or have had a qualifying life event.
Otherwise you must wait for the renewal on 1 July.

**Q6. How long is my notice period?**
Sixty calendar days if you are at Band B1 to B3, and ninety calendar days at Band B4 and
above. Thirty calendar days during probation.

**Q7. I did not submit investment proofs in January. What happens?**
The provisional declaration is withdrawn and tax is recomputed without those deductions,
with the shortfall recovered from the February and March payrolls. You may still claim the
deduction when you file your return of income.

**Q8. Why is my professional tax INR 300 in February?**
Maharashtra levies INR 200 for ten months and INR 300 in February, totalling INR 2,500 for
the year. Haryana does not levy professional tax at all.
