## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This Manual describes how Nexora Technologies brings a new person into the organisation and
how it develops them thereafter. It covers the period from offer acceptance to the end of the
first year, and the ongoing learning framework that continues for the whole of an employee's
career at Nexora.

It is written for four audiences, and each clause states who is accountable:

| Audience | What this Manual gives them |
|---|---|
| The new joiner | What will happen, when, and what they must do |
| The hiring manager | A day-by-day checklist and the outcomes they own |
| The onboarding buddy | The scope and boundaries of the buddy role |
| HR Operations, IT and Facilities | The service levels they are held to |

### 1.2 The Standard We Hold Ourselves To

Nexora measures onboarding against five outcomes. These are reported to the ELT each quarter.

| Outcome | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Ready on day one | Laptop, accounts and seat available at 09:30 on the date of joining | 98% |
| Productive quickly | First meaningful contribution merged, shipped or delivered | Within 21 calendar days |
| Compliant | All mandatory courses completed | Within 30 days of joining |
| Connected | New joiner can name their manager, buddy, HRBP and skip-level | 100% at day 30 |
| Retained | Voluntary attrition in the first 180 days | Below 4% |

### 1.3 Scope

This Manual applies to all new joiners at Gurugram, Bengaluru and Pune: lateral hires,
Graduate Engineer Trainees, fixed-term contract employees, interns, and employees rejoining
after a break. Sections 9 and 10 apply additionally to internal transfers and to employees
returning from extended leave.

The DOJ − 3 readiness check is a gate, not a status update. Where any item is red, the HR
Operations lead escalates to the function head the same day. Onboarding a person to a desk
with no laptop is treated as a service failure and is reported in the quarterly metrics.

### 2.2 The Welcome Call

The hiring manager calls the new joiner seven days before the date of joining. The call is not
administrative; HR Operations handles administration separately. The manager covers:

1. What the team is working on right now, in plain language.
2. Who the new joiner will sit with and who their buddy will be.
3. What the first week will look like.
4. What the first thirty days are meant to achieve.
5. Anything the new joiner is anxious about — relocation, notice-period overlap, a family
   commitment in the first month.

Managers record the completion of the welcome call on PeopleHub. Where a new joiner cannot be
reached after three attempts across two days, the Talent Acquisition partner is informed, as
this is the strongest early indicator of a candidate who will not join.

A new joiner who has not uploaded documents 1, 2 and 9 by the date of joining can be onboarded
but cannot be added to the payroll master, and will miss the first payroll cut-off if the
documents arrive after the 18th.

### 2.4 Background Verification

BGV is initiated at DOJ − 21 and runs in parallel with onboarding. A joiner starts work while
BGV is in progress, on the express condition recorded in the appointment letter that the
appointment is conditional on a clear result.

| Result | Meaning | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Green | All checks clear | Recorded; confirmation proceeds normally |
| Amber | Minor discrepancy — a date, a title, an unverifiable small employer | Panel review: HRBP, hiring manager, Legal; usually closed with an explanation on file |
| Red | Material discrepancy — fabricated employment, forged qualification, undisclosed conviction, undisclosed overlapping employment | Offer withdrawn, or services terminated with immediate effect under HR-POL-001, Clause 15.3 |

A joiner whose relieving letter is not produced within ninety (90) days of joining is treated
as a Red finding, because Nexora cannot then establish that the previous employment has ended.

## 4. Induction Week

Induction Week runs from day one to day five and is common to all new joiners at all
locations. It is delivered in a blend of in-person sessions at the site Learning Hub and
self-paced modules on NexoraLearn. Attendance is mandatory; a session missed for a genuine
reason is made up in the following month's cohort.

### 4.1 Induction Week Schedule

| Day | Morning | Afternoon | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome, documentation, IT setup | Policy, security, site orientation, team intro | Day-one checklist closed |
| 2 | Nexora: history, clients, practices, delivery model | Engineering standards and the Nexora way of working | Practice overview quiz |
| 3 | Information Security Awareness (NEX-SEC-101); ISMS overview (NEX-SEC-107) | Data protection and the DPDP Act (NEX-LEG-104) | 3 mandatory courses complete |
| 4 | Code of Conduct (NEX-HR-103); POSH orientation (NEX-HR-102) | Anti-Bribery (NEX-LEG-105); ethics case discussion | 3 mandatory courses complete |
| 5 | Quality Management System (NEX-QMS-106); tools walkthrough | 30-60-90 plan agreed with the manager; buddy check-in | Signed 30-60-90 plan on PeopleHub |

### 4.2 Mandatory Compliance Courses

All seven courses must be completed within **thirty (30) calendar days** of joining. Induction
Week is designed so that a new joiner completes all seven within the first five days.

| Course code | Title | Duration | Assessment | Pass mark | Renewal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NEX-SEC-101 | Information Security Awareness | 2.0 hours | 20 questions | 80% | Annual |
| NEX-HR-102 | Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) | 2.0 hours | 15 questions | 80% | Annual |
| NEX-HR-103 | Code of Conduct and Business Ethics | 1.5 hours | 15 questions | 80% | Annual |

## 5. The Buddy Programme

### 5.1 Purpose

Every new joiner is assigned a buddy for the first thirty (30) days. The buddy is the person a
new joiner asks the questions they would not ask their manager: where the good coffee is, what
"ODC" stands for, whether it is normal to still feel lost in week two, who actually knows how
the deployment pipeline works.

### 5.2 Who Can Be a Buddy

A buddy must be a confirmed employee, in the same team or an adjacent team, at the same band or
one band above the new joiner, with at least six months at Nexora, not in the new joiner's
reporting line, and not carrying more than one buddy assignment at a time.

A buddy assignment is recognised: the buddy receives an allowance of **INR 3,000** on
completion of the thirty-day period and a positive closing note from the new joiner, and the
assignment is recorded against the buddy's people-contribution goal in the performance review.

### 5.3 What a Buddy Does

| Timing | Buddy action |
|---|---|
| DOJ − 2 | Sends a personal welcome message on the new joiner's personal email or phone |
| Day 1 | Lunch; site walk-through; introductions to at least five people |
| Days 2 – 5 | Daily fifteen-minute check-in |
| Week 2 | Two check-ins; walks through the team's tools and rituals |
| Weeks 3 – 4 | Weekly check-in; introduces the new joiner to adjacent teams |
| Day 30 | Closing conversation; buddy submits a short note to the manager and HRBP |

### 5.4 What a Buddy Does Not Do

A buddy does not set goals, assess performance, approve leave, handle a grievance, or discuss
compensation. A buddy who becomes aware of a wellbeing concern, a policy breach or a POSH
matter refers it to the HRBP rather than handling it; the referral itself is not a breach of
the new joiner's confidence and must be made.

## 6. The 30-60-90 Day Plan

### 6.1 Requirement

Every new joiner has a written 30-60-90 day plan, drafted by the hiring manager before the
date of joining, agreed with the new joiner by the end of week one, and recorded on
PeopleHub → Onboarding → 30-60-90. The plan is reviewed at day 30, day 60 and day 90, and each
review is recorded.

### 6.2 Structure

| Horizon | Theme | Typical outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1 – 30 | Learn | Understand the client, the product, the architecture, the team's rituals and the delivery model. Complete all mandatory training. Ship one small, real change end to end. Meet ten named people. |
| Days 31 – 60 | Contribute | Own a component, a test suite, a service or a workstream. Participate fully in estimation, review and stand-up. Resolve production or client queries with light supervision. Present once to the team. |
| Days 61 – 90 | Own | Deliver an agreed piece of work independently and on schedule. Improve something nobody asked you to improve. Mentor or assist a newer joiner. Agree development goals for the rest of the year. |

### 6.3 Illustrative Plan — Senior Software Engineer (Band B3), BFSI Practice, Gurugram

**Days 1 – 30**
1. Complete all seven mandatory compliance courses (day 5).
2. Obtain client environment access; complete client-mandated training on payments data
   handling (day 12).
3. Set up the local development environment and run the full test suite successfully (day 7).
4. Read and summarise the solution architecture document for the Settlement Services platform;
   present the summary at the team stand-up (day 15).
5. Pick up and merge three defects of complexity "Low" from the backlog (day 25).
6. Meet the Delivery Manager, the client's technical lead, the QE lead, the architect, and four
   team members; record notes (day 30).

**Days 31 – 60**
1. Take ownership of the Reconciliation Engine module, including its on-call runbook (day 40).
2. Deliver the ISO 20022 message mapping change end to end: design note, implementation, unit
   and integration tests, code review, deployment to UAT (day 55).
3. Participate in sprint estimation and lead the estimation for at least two stories (day 45).
4. Complete the first buddy-independent client status call (day 50).
5. Reduce the module's flaky test count from eleven to zero (day 60).

**Days 61 – 90**
1. Own delivery of the Q3 statement-generation enhancement, from refinement to production
   (day 85).
2. Join the on-call roster following on-call shadowing of two rotations (day 70).
3. Propose and implement one improvement to the CI pipeline; measure the change in build time
   (day 80).
4. Onboard and buddy the next joiner to the team (day 90).
5. Agree performance goals and the Individual Development Plan for the remainder of the year
   with the manager (day 90).

### 6.4 Review Gates

| Gate | Participants | Recorded outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Day 30 | New joiner, manager | On track / needs support; training complete or not |
| Day 60 | New joiner, manager, buddy (closing) | On track / needs support; buddy programme closed |
| Day 90 | New joiner, manager, HRBP | Formal probation review 1; feeds the confirmation decision |
| Day 180 | New joiner, manager, HRBP | Formal probation review 2; confirmation decision |

## 7. Nexora Launchpad — The Graduate Engineer Trainee Programme

### 7.1 Overview

Nexora Launchpad is the eight-week structured training programme for Graduate Engineer
Trainees hired from campus. Cohorts start in July and January each year. The 2026 July cohort
is expected to be 120 trainees across the three sites, with the majority at Bengaluru and
Pune.

GETs are on the Nexora payroll from day one at the GET compensation band (B1 entry), receive
full benefits other than variable pay, and carry a service commitment of eighteen (18) months
from the date of joining as recorded in the GET appointment letter.

### 7.2 Programme Structure

| Phase | Weeks | Focus | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 1 – 3 | Programming fundamentals, data structures, version control, SQL, Linux, working in a team, written communication | Weekly coding assessment; pass mark 60% |
| Technical Track | 4 – 7 | One of four specialisations: Java/Spring, .NET, Data Engineering, Cloud and DevOps | Track assessment; pass mark 65% |
| Capstone | 8 | Team project: build, test and deploy a small application against a written brief | Panel evaluation; pass mark 60% |

### 7.3 Foundation Phase — Weeks 1 to 3

| Week | Content | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nexora induction and mandatory compliance courses; Git and the Nexora branching model; Linux command line; IDE and debugging | 40 |
| 2 | Data structures and algorithms; complexity; problem-solving workshops; unit testing and test-driven development | 40 |
| 3 | Relational databases and SQL; REST API design; HTTP and web fundamentals; written communication for engineers; agile ways of working | 40 |

### 7.4 Technical Tracks — Weeks 4 to 7

| Track | Core content |
|---|---|
| Java / Spring | Java 21 language features, Spring Boot, JPA and Hibernate, Maven, JUnit and Mockito, Docker, REST and messaging |
| .NET | C# 12, ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework Core, NuGet, xUnit, Docker, REST and messaging |
| Data Engineering | Python, advanced SQL, dimensional modelling, Airflow, Spark, dbt, data quality and data contracts |
| Cloud and DevOps | AWS and Azure core services, Terraform, Kubernetes, CI/CD with GitLab, observability, incident response |

### 7.5 Capstone — Week 8

Teams of four to five trainees build a working application against a brief issued on the
Monday morning and demonstrate it on the Friday afternoon to a panel of a practice architect,
a delivery manager and an L&D facilitator. Evaluation covers functionality against the brief
(30%), code quality and test coverage (25%), architecture and design decisions (20%),
demonstration and communication (15%), and teamwork as observed by the facilitator (10%).

### 7.6 Progression and Non-completion

| Situation | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Passes all three phases | Deployed to a project; six-month probation begins from the deployment date |
| Fails one weekly assessment | Remedial support; reassessment within the following week |
| Fails a phase assessment | One reassessment opportunity within 10 working days |
| Fails a reassessment | Placed in the next cohort's corresponding phase, once only |
| Fails after repeating a phase | Services discontinued with 30 days' notice under HR-POL-001, Clause 3.4 |
| Voluntary exit before the end of the service commitment | Training cost recovery of INR 1,00,000, pro-rated over the 18 months |

### 7.7 Support During Launchpad

Each cohort of fifteen trainees is assigned a dedicated facilitator, a technical mentor from
the practice, and a Launchpad HRBP. Weekly one-to-ones with the facilitator are mandatory.
Trainees relocating for the programme receive relocation assistance under HR-POL-001,
Clause 7.6, and, where required, up to thirty (30) nights of Company-arranged shared
accommodation during weeks one to four.

## 8. The Learning Framework

### 8.1 The Annual Commitment

Every employee is expected to complete a minimum of **forty (40) learning hours per financial
year**, of which:

- at least sixteen (16) hours on the primary technical or functional track;
- the mandatory compliance courses in Clause 4.2, which count towards the total;
- at least four (4) hours on behavioural, communication or leadership content for Band B3 and
  above.

Learning hours are recorded automatically on NexoraLearn for internal content, and are
recorded by the employee with evidence for external content. They appear in the performance
record and are one input to the annual rating.

### 8.2 The Course Catalogue

maintained by the Learning and Development team, reviewed each quarter, and published on

financial year, and delivery hours are recorded against the faculty member's
people-contribution goal.

As at 1 February 2026 Nexora has 63 active internal faculty members delivering 34 of the 60
catalogue courses.

## 9. Onboarding for Internal Moves and Returners

### 9.1 Internal Transfers

An employee moving to a new team or function goes through a shortened onboarding: no Induction
Week, no buddy allowance, but a mandatory 30-60-90 day plan, a named point of contact in the
new team, and a role-specific track from Clause 4.3 where the role family changes.

Access changes must be requested through NexServe by the receiving manager and the releasing
manager jointly. Access to the previous team's systems and client environments is revoked
within **twenty-four (24) hours** of the effective transfer date. This is audited monthly by
the Information Security team; standing access after a transfer is a reportable finding.

### 9.2 Return from Extended Leave

An employee returning from maternity leave, sabbatical, or medical absence exceeding thirty
(30) days is re-onboarded through a structured return:

| Step | Timing | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-return conversation: role, hours, phased return, any accommodation needed | 2 weeks before return | Manager, HRBP |
| System access reactivated; device reissued if surrendered | 2 days before return | IT |
| Re-orientation: what changed in the team, the client and the product | Day 1 | Manager |
| Mandatory compliance courses refreshed where they lapsed during the absence | Week 1 | Employee |
| Phased return where agreed — reduced hours or reduced scope for up to 8 weeks | Weeks 1 – 8 | Manager |
| Check-in on the return experience | Day 30 | HRBP |

An employee returning from maternity leave has the additional entitlements set out in
HR-POL-004, Clause 7.1, including nursing breaks and the option of working from home for up to
twelve weeks.

### 9.3 Onboarding to a New Client Engagement

Rotation onto a new client engagement requires:

1. Client-specific NDA signed and recorded.
2. Client-mandated training completed — typically information security, and, in regulated
   sectors, sector-specific modules on payments, healthcare data or market abuse.
3. Client background check where the client's contract requires one beyond Nexora's own.
4. Access provisioned by the client under a named business justification.
5. Delivery Operating Model briefing by the Delivery Manager: working hours, shift pattern,
   escalation path, communication protocol, data handling constraints, and whether AI-assisted
   development is permitted for that client.
