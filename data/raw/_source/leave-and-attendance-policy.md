## 1. Purpose, Scope and Principles

### 1.1 Purpose

This Policy establishes a single, uniform framework for leave entitlement, leave
administration, attendance recording and absence management across Nexora Technologies
Private Limited. It replaces all earlier location-specific practices, including the separate
Bengaluru attendance addendum of March 2019 and the Pune shift-attendance note of July 2022,
both of which stand withdrawn with effect from 1 January 2026.

### 1.2 Scope

This Policy applies to all employees on the Nexora payroll, including probationers, Graduate
Engineer Trainees after deployment, and fixed-term contract employees. It does not apply to
interns engaged for fewer than ninety (90) days, to independent contractors, or to
staffing-partner deputees, whose absence arrangements are governed by their own contracts.

Where a provision of this Policy is less favourable than a mandatory requirement of the
Shops and Commercial Establishments legislation of Haryana, Karnataka or Maharashtra, or of
any other applicable statute, the statutory provision prevails to the extent of the
shortfall, and the HR Operations team will apply the statutory position without requiring
an application from the employee.

### 1.3 Governing Principles

1. **Leave is planned.** Except for sickness and genuine emergency, leave is applied for and
   approved before it is taken.
2. **Leave is an entitlement, not a favour.** A manager may decline leave only for a
   documented business reason and must offer an alternative window.
3. **Attendance is a record, not a surveillance instrument.** Attendance data is used for
   payroll, statutory registers, client billing and capacity planning. It is not used to
   evaluate individual performance in isolation.
4. **Rest is not optional.** Managers are accountable for ensuring that their team members
   take leave. An employee carrying more than twenty-five (25) days of unused Earned Leave
   on 30 September triggers an alert to the manager and the HRBP.

### 1.4 The Leave Year

The Nexora leave year runs from **1 January to 31 December**. It deliberately differs from
the financial year (1 April to 31 March) and from the performance year (1 April to
31 March). Leave balances, carry-forward, lapse and encashment are all calculated on the
calendar year.

## 2. Leave Entitlements

### 2.1 Summary of Entitlements

| # | Leave type | Code | Annual entitlement | Credited | Carry forward | Encashable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Casual Leave | CL | 12 days | 3 days on 1 Jan, 1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct | No | No |
| 2 | Sick Leave | SL | 12 days | 12 days on 1 January | No | No |
| 3 | Earned Leave | EL | 18 days | 1.5 days per completed calendar month | Up to 30 days | Yes |
| 4 | Compensatory Off | CO | As earned | On approved holiday/weekly-off work | No | No |
| 5 | Maternity Leave | ML | 26 weeks | On application | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| 6 | Paternity Leave | PL | 10 working days | On application | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| 7 | Adoption Leave | AL | 12 weeks | On application | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| 8 | Bereavement Leave | BL | 5 calendar days per event | On application | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| 9 | Marriage Leave | MRL | 5 working days, once in tenure | On application | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| 10 | Loss of Pay | LOP | Not applicable | Not applicable | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| 11 | Sabbatical | SAB | Up to 6 months, unpaid | On approval | Not applicable | Not applicable |
| 12 | Special Leave | SPL | At Company discretion | On approval | Not applicable | Not applicable |

**Total paid annual leave for a confirmed employee: 42 days (12 CL + 12 SL + 18 EL),
exclusive of public holidays and statutory parental leave.**

### 2.2 Pro-rata Entitlement in the Year of Joining

Casual Leave and Sick Leave are credited pro rata from the month of joining. Earned Leave
accrues from the first completed calendar month of service. The joining-date rule is:

| Date of joining in a month | Credit for that month |
|---|---|
| 1st to 15th | Full month credited |
| 16th to end of month | No credit for that month; accrual begins the following month |

Worked example: an employee joining on 12 August 2026 is credited 1.25 days of CL and 1.25
days of SL for August, plus the pro-rated balance for the remainder of the year, giving
5 days of CL and 5 days of SL for the period August to December 2026. Earned Leave accrues
at 1.5 days for each of August, September, October, November and December, giving 7.5 days,
rounded up to 8 days at the 31 December closing.

A second worked example: an employee joining on 20 August 2026 receives no credit for
August. CL and SL are credited at 1 day per month for September to December, giving 4 days
each, and Earned Leave accrues 6 days.

### 2.3 Rounding

All balances are maintained to two decimal places in PeopleHub and are rounded to the
nearest half-day only at the point of application. A balance of 0.25 day or less cannot be
applied and is carried in the ledger until it accumulates.

## 3. Casual Leave (CL)

### 3.1 Purpose and Quantum

Casual Leave covers short, unplanned personal absences: a domestic emergency, a civic
appointment, a school event, a vehicle breakdown, a religious observance not covered by the
holiday calendar. The entitlement is twelve (12) days per leave year, credited three (3)
days on the first day of each quarter.

### 3.2 Rules

1. Casual Leave may be taken in units of half a day or one full day.
2. Not more than **three (3) consecutive days** of Casual Leave may be taken at one time.
   An absence longer than three days must be applied as Earned Leave.
3. Casual Leave may not be combined with Earned Leave in a single continuous absence, except
   where the Casual Leave is the first or last day of the absence and the total absence does
   not exceed five (5) days.
4. Casual Leave may be prefixed or suffixed to a weekly off or a public holiday. Intervening
   weekly offs and holidays are not counted against the Casual Leave balance.
5. Unused Casual Leave lapses on 31 December. It is not carried forward and is not
   encashable, on separation or otherwise.
6. Casual Leave credited for a quarter that has not yet begun may not be availed in advance.

### 3.3 Notice

Casual Leave requires a minimum of one (1) working day's advance notice on PeopleHub. Where
the circumstances make advance notice impossible, the employee must inform the reporting
manager by telephone or message before 10:00 IST on the day of absence and must regularise
the application on PeopleHub on the first working day after return.

## 4. Sick Leave (SL)

### 4.1 Purpose and Quantum

Sick Leave covers absence caused by the employee's own illness, injury, medical procedure,
or a medically advised period of quarantine or recovery. The entitlement is twelve (12) days
per leave year, credited in full on 1 January.

### 4.2 Rules

1. Sick Leave may be taken in units of half a day or one full day.
2. Sick Leave for **more than two (2) consecutive working days** must be supported by a
   medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner, stating the period of advised
   rest. The certificate is uploaded to PeopleHub within seven (7) calendar days of return.
3. Sick Leave for **more than five (5) consecutive working days** additionally requires a
   fitness-to-resume certificate before the employee returns to work.
4. Sick Leave for **more than ten (10) consecutive working days** is reviewed by the HRBP,
   who may, with the employee's consent, refer the case to the Company's medical adviser and
   may convert the balance of the absence to Earned Leave or to Special Leave.
5. Sick Leave may not be applied prospectively except for a scheduled medical procedure, for
   which the application may be made in advance with supporting documentation.
6. Sick Leave may be used for the hospitalisation of a spouse, dependent child or dependent
   parent, up to a maximum of five (5) days in a leave year, on production of the discharge
   summary or admission record.
7. Unused Sick Leave lapses on 31 December. It is not carried forward and is not encashable.

### 4.3 Exhausted Sick Leave

Where an employee's Sick Leave is exhausted, further medical absence is applied against
Earned Leave, and thereafter against Casual Leave, and thereafter as Loss of Pay. In cases
of serious illness supported by medical documentation, the CHRO may sanction Special Leave
on full or half pay for up to sixty (60) days in a leave year.

## 5. Earned Leave (EL)

### 5.1 Purpose and Quantum

Earned Leave, also referred to as Privilege Leave, is intended for planned rest and personal
commitments requiring an extended absence. The entitlement is eighteen (18) days per leave
year, accruing at one and a half (1.5) days for each completed calendar month of service.

### 5.2 Rules

1. Earned Leave may be taken in units of half a day or one full day. There is no maximum
   duration, subject to approval.
2. Earned Leave of three (3) or more consecutive days requires seven (7) calendar days'
   advance notice. Earned Leave of five (5) or more consecutive days requires fifteen (15)
   calendar days' advance notice. Earned Leave of ten (10) or more consecutive days requires
   thirty (30) calendar days' advance notice and the additional approval of the Delivery
   Manager and the HRBP.
3. Earned Leave may not be availed in advance of accrual. An employee whose accrued balance
   is insufficient may apply for the shortfall as Loss of Pay.
4. Weekly offs and public holidays falling within a period of Earned Leave **are counted**
   against the Earned Leave balance where the leave period exceeds five (5) continuous
   calendar days. For absences of five days or fewer, intervening weekly offs and holidays
   are not counted.
5. An employee on Earned Leave for fifteen (15) or more continuous days must complete a
   documented handover to a named backup, recorded on PeopleHub before the leave begins.

### 5.3 Carry Forward and Lapse

A maximum of **thirty (30) days** of unused Earned Leave may be carried forward from one
leave year to the next. The carry-forward is computed at the 31 December closing.

Any Earned Leave balance in excess of thirty (30) days on 31 December is **automatically
encashed** in the January payroll of the following year at the rate of:

> Encashment per day = (Monthly Basic Salary + Dearness Allowance) ÷ 30

Automatic encashment is a payroll event; no application is required. It is taxable as salary
income and is reflected in the payslip under "EL Encashment".

Worked example: an employee with a monthly Basic of INR 62,000 and no Dearness Allowance
holds 37 days of Earned Leave on 31 December 2026. Thirty days are carried forward to 2027;
seven days are encashed at INR 2,066.67 per day, giving INR 14,466.69, credited in the
January 2027 payroll.

### 5.4 Encashment on Separation

On separation, accrued and unused Earned Leave up to a maximum of thirty (30) days is
encashed in the full and final settlement at the rate in Clause 5.3, computed on the last
drawn Basic Salary. Earned Leave in excess of thirty days at the date of separation lapses.

Earned Leave may **not** be adjusted against the notice period. Where an employee takes
approved leave during the notice period, the last working day is extended by an equivalent
number of calendar days, unless the function head waives the extension in writing.

### 5.5 Leave Recall

The Company reserves the right to recall an employee from Earned Leave in the event of a
declared client emergency or a Severity-1 production incident. Where an employee is recalled:

(a) the days not availed are re-credited to the leave balance;
(b) any non-refundable travel or accommodation cost actually incurred by the employee is
    reimbursed on production of the invoice, up to INR 60,000; and
(c) the recall must be approved by a Band B6 or above leader and recorded on PeopleHub.

Recall is exceptional. A function recording more than four recalls in a rolling twelve-month
period is reviewed by the COO.

## 6. Compensatory Off (CO)

### 6.1 Eligibility

A Compensatory Off is credited where an employee, at the documented request of the reporting
manager, works on a weekly off or a public holiday. It is not credited for extended hours on
a normal working day, nor for voluntary weekend work undertaken without a prior request.

| Hours worked on a weekly off or holiday | Compensatory Off credited |
|---|---|
| 4 hours or more but less than 8 hours | 0.5 day |
| 8 hours or more | 1 day |
| Less than 4 hours | Nil |

### 6.2 Rules

1. The manager must raise the Compensatory Off request on PeopleHub **before** the weekend
   or holiday work, or within three (3) working days after it where the work arose from an
   unplanned incident.
2. A Compensatory Off must be availed within **sixty (60) days** of the date on which it was
   earned. An unavailed Compensatory Off lapses at the end of that period and is not
   encashable under any circumstances.
3. A maximum of six (6) Compensatory Offs may be held in the balance at any time. Where the
   balance would exceed six, the manager must schedule the employee's time off before
   approving further weekend work.
4. Compensatory Off may be combined with Casual Leave or Earned Leave.
5. Employees rostered on a shift pattern whose weekly offs fall on days other than Saturday
   and Sunday earn Compensatory Off in respect of their rostered weekly offs, not in respect
   of Saturday and Sunday.
6. Compensatory Off is not available to employees at Band B6 and above, whose terms
   contemplate the flexibility required by the role.

## 7. Parental and Family Leave

### 7.1 Maternity Leave

Maternity Leave is granted in accordance with the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961, as amended.

| Circumstance | Entitlement |
|---|---|
| First and second surviving child | 26 weeks, of which up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected date of delivery |
| Third and subsequent child | 12 weeks, of which up to 6 weeks may be taken before the expected date of delivery |
| Adoptive mother (child below 3 months at handover) | 12 weeks from the date the child is handed over |
| Commissioning mother | 12 weeks from the date the child is handed over |
| Miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy | 6 weeks from the date of the event |
| Tubectomy | 2 weeks from the date of the operation |
| Illness arising out of pregnancy, delivery, premature birth or termination | An additional 1 month, on medical certificate |

**Administration.** The employee notifies the HRBP and the reporting manager at least eight
(8) weeks before the intended start of leave, and uploads the medical certificate stating
the expected date of delivery. Full salary is paid throughout Maternity Leave. Provident
Fund, insurance cover and Earned Leave accrual continue without interruption.

**Return to work.** On return, the employee is entitled to be placed in the same role, or a
role of equivalent band, scope and compensation. The employee is entitled to two nursing
breaks of fifteen (15) minutes each per day, in addition to the meal break, until the child
attains the age of fifteen (15) months.

**Work from home after Maternity Leave.** The employee may elect to work from home for a
further period of up to twelve (12) weeks immediately after Maternity Leave, on full pay,
subject to the nature of the work permitting it, in accordance with Section 5(5) of the
Maternity Benefit Act, 1961.

**Creche.** Nexora reimburses creche expenses of up to INR 8,000 per month per child for
children up to six (6) years of age, for a maximum of twenty-four (24) months per child. The
Bengaluru and Gurugram sites additionally have a tie-up with the Little Orbit creche network
within 2 km of the office.

### 7.2 Paternity Leave

Paternity Leave of **ten (10) working days** is granted to an employee on the birth of a
child, or on the legal adoption of a child below the age of three (3) years.

1. Paternity Leave may be taken in a maximum of two blocks, within six (6) months of the
   date of birth or the date of handover of the adopted child.
2. Paternity Leave is available for up to two children during the employee's tenure with the
   Company.
3. The application must be supported by the birth certificate or the adoption order, uploaded
   to PeopleHub within thirty (30) days of the start of the leave.
4. Paternity Leave is available to all employees irrespective of gender who are the
   non-birthing parent, including partners in a same-sex relationship recognised by the
   employee's nomination on PeopleHub.

### 7.3 Bereavement Leave

Five (5) calendar days per event, on the death of a spouse, child, parent, parent-in-law,
sibling or grandparent. The leave is granted on intimation to the reporting manager; no
prior approval is required, and no documentation is demanded, though HR may request a copy
of the death certificate where the leave exceeds five days and is extended into Special
Leave. Travel time for an employee whose family is in another State may be added by the HRBP
up to two (2) additional days.

### 7.4 Marriage Leave

Five (5) working days, once during the employee's tenure with the Company, on the occasion of
the employee's own marriage. The application must be made at least thirty (30) days in
advance and must be accompanied by the wedding invitation or, after the event, the marriage
certificate.

### 7.5 Sabbatical

Employees who have completed five (5) years of continuous service may apply for an unpaid
sabbatical of up to six (6) months for higher education, care of a dependant, medical
recovery, or a defined personal project.

| Aspect | Treatment during sabbatical |
|---|---|
| Salary | Not paid |
| Group Mediclaim, GPA, GTL | Continue; employee bears the premium for the sabbatical months |
| Provident Fund | No contribution by either party |
| Earned Leave accrual | Suspended |
| Gratuity service | Counted as continuous service |
| Role on return | Same band; specific role subject to availability |

Sabbatical requires the approval of the function head and the CHRO, must be applied for at
least sixty (60) days in advance, and carries a service commitment of twelve (12) months on
return. Not more than 2% of a function's headcount may be on sabbatical at any one time.

## 8. Attendance

### 8.1 Working Hours

The standard work week is Monday to Friday, nine (9) hours per day inclusive of a sixty (60)
minute meal break, giving forty (40) net working hours per week. Employees may start work at
any time between 08:00 and 11:00 IST and must be available during core hours of 11:00 to
17:00 IST.

### 8.2 Recording

Attendance is recorded through the access-card swipe at the office turnstile and through the
PeopleHub check-in, which may be completed from the Nexora network, through the PeopleHub
mobile application with location services enabled, or through the VPN client for employees
working remotely on an approved day.

Both records must exist for a day to be counted automatically. Where only one exists, the
day appears as an exception on the manager's PeopleHub dashboard and must be regularised.

### 8.3 Day Classification

| Net hours | Classification | Payroll effect |
|---|---|---|
| 8.00 and above | Present — full day | Nil |
| 6.00 to 7.99 | Short day | Flagged; three in a month trigger manager review |
| 4.50 to 5.99 | Half day | 0.5 day leave deducted |
| Below 4.50 | Absent | 1 day leave deducted |

### 8.4 Regularisation

Regularisation requests are raised on PeopleHub within seven (7) calendar days of the date in
question, with a reason and, where applicable, evidence such as a client meeting invitation or
a travel record. A maximum of three (3) regularisations per calendar month is permitted
without HRBP approval. The monthly attendance register locks on the 18th for the preceding
period; entries after the lock are adjusted in the following month's payroll.

### 8.5 Late Marks and Early Departure

An arrival after 11:00 IST without a prior approved exception is a late mark. A departure
before the completion of net eight hours, other than on approved half-day leave, is an early
departure and is treated in the same manner as a late mark.

| Late marks / early departures in a calendar month | Consequence |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | No action |
| 3 to 5 | Deduction of 0.5 day Casual Leave |
| 6 to 8 | Deduction of 1 day Casual Leave and a counselling note by the manager |
| More than 8 | Attendance deficiency; formal counselling under HR-POL-001, Clause 15.2 |

Late marks are not recorded for employees on an approved shift roster, on client-site
deputation, on approved business travel, on an approved medical appointment, or where the
Facilities team has notified a transport disruption, civic emergency, or a weather event
affecting the site.

### 8.6 Minimum Monthly Attendance

An employee must record a minimum of **eighteen (18) attended days** in a calendar month
containing twenty-one or more working days, counting approved paid leave as attended, in
order for the month to count as a full month for Earned Leave accrual and for variable pay
computation. Months containing Maternity Leave, Paternity Leave or a Company-approved
sabbatical are excluded from this rule.

calendar comprises:

- **Ten (10) fixed holidays** observed at all three locations;
- **One or two regional holidays** specific to each location, reflecting State practice;
- **Two (2) optional holidays** selected by each employee from a published list of eligible
  dates, declared on PeopleHub by 31 January.

### 9.2 Rules

1. A fixed or regional holiday that falls on a Saturday or a Sunday is not compensated with
   an alternative working-day holiday.
2. Optional holidays must be declared in advance on PeopleHub. An optional holiday not
   declared and not taken by 31 December lapses.
3. An employee who works on a fixed or regional holiday at the manager's documented request

4. An employee on deputation to another Nexora location for more than thirty (30) continuous
   days observes the holiday calendar of the host location for the duration of the

5. Employees deputed to a client site observe the Nexora calendar unless the engagement's

## 10. Leave Administration

### 10.1 The Application Workflow

| Step | Actor | System | Service level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employee raises leave request with dates, type and reason | PeopleHub → My Leave → Apply | — |
| 2 | Reporting manager approves, declines with reason, or requests modification | PeopleHub → Team → Approvals | 2 working days |
| 3 | Second-level approval where required (leave of 10+ days, sabbatical, special leave) | Delivery Manager and HRBP | 3 working days |
| 4 | Balance updated; calendar and project resource plan updated | PeopleHub, automatic | Immediate on approval |
| 5 | Escalation where no action in 2 working days | Auto-escalation to skip-level manager | On day 3 |

A leave request that receives no decision within five (5) working days is auto-approved by
PeopleHub, and the approval is logged against the skip-level manager. Auto-approval does not
apply to sabbatical, special leave or leave of more than fifteen (15) days.

### 10.2 Declining Leave

A manager who declines a leave request must record a business reason in the PeopleHub
comment field and must propose at least one alternative window within the following sixty
(60) days. Generic reasons such as "project pressure" are not acceptable; the reason must
identify the specific delivery commitment at risk.

A leave request may not be declined where:

(a) the request is for Sick Leave supported by a medical certificate;
(b) the request is for Maternity, Paternity, Adoption, Bereavement or statutory leave;
(c) the employee has been declined the same window twice previously; or
(d) declining would cause the employee's Earned Leave balance to exceed the thirty-day
    carry-forward cap on 31 December.

### 10.3 Cancellation and Modification

Approved leave may be cancelled by the employee up to the day before the leave begins,
directly on PeopleHub. Cancellation of leave already commenced requires manager approval and
is effective from the date of return to work. Leave cancelled after the start date is
re-credited only for the days not availed.

### 10.4 Leave During Notice Period

Leave during the notice period requires the approval of the function head in addition to the
reporting manager. Approved leave during notice extends the last working day by an equivalent
number of calendar days, unless the function head expressly waives the extension in writing
on PeopleHub. Casual Leave and Sick Leave credited but unused at the date of resignation may
be availed during the notice period subject to this clause; they are not encashable.

### 10.5 Leave During Probation

Probationers accrue and may avail Casual Leave and Sick Leave from the date of joining.
Earned Leave accrues during probation but may be availed only after confirmation, except with
the approval of the HRBP in a documented emergency. Leave taken during probation in excess of
five (5) days may result in the extension of probation by an equivalent period.

### 10.6 Records and Retention

Leave records are maintained on PeopleHub and retained for eight (8) years from the end of
the leave year, in accordance with the record-retention requirements of the applicable Shops
and Commercial Establishments rules. Employees may download their own leave ledger at any
time from PeopleHub → My Leave → Ledger.

The statutory leave register in the prescribed form for each State is generated from
PeopleHub by HR Operations and is maintained at each site for inspection.

## 11. Absence Management

### 11.1 Unauthorised Absence

Absence without an approved leave application and without intimation to the reporting manager
is unauthorised absence. It is recorded as Loss of Pay and follows this escalation:

| Consecutive working days absent | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manager attempts contact; notes attempt on PeopleHub | Reporting manager |
| 3 | Formal contact attempt by email and registered mobile; HRBP informed | Reporting manager |
| 5 | Written show-cause notice by email and registered post, requiring response in 7 days | HR Operations |
| 8 | Second and final notice; access suspended pending response | HR Operations |
| 10 | Deemed voluntary abandonment of service; record closed with effect from day 1 | CHRO |

Where an employee provides a satisfactory explanation, the absence may be regularised against
available leave balances or as Loss of Pay, at the HRBP's determination.

### 11.2 Loss of Pay Computation

Loss of Pay is deducted at:

> LOP per day = Monthly Gross Salary ÷ Number of calendar days in the month

Loss of Pay affects Provident Fund contribution, which is computed on the reduced Basic
Salary for the month, and affects the Earned Leave accrual for that month where the
requirement of Clause 8.6 is not met. Loss of Pay of more than five (5) days in a calendar
month is reported to the HRBP and reviewed with the manager.

### 11.3 Absenteeism Patterns

HR Operations reports the following patterns to the HRBP each month for review with the
manager. Identification of a pattern is not, by itself, evidence of misconduct; it prompts a
supportive conversation first.

1. Casual Leave or Sick Leave adjoining a weekly off or public holiday on three or more
   occasions in a rolling quarter.
2. Single-day Sick Leave on a Monday or a Friday on four or more occasions in a rolling
   six-month period.
3. Exhaustion of the full Sick Leave entitlement before 30 June.
4. Repeated regularisation requests at the seven-day boundary.

### 11.4 Extended Medical Absence

An employee absent on medical grounds for more than thirty (30) continuous calendar days is
placed on the Extended Medical Absence register maintained by the HRBP. During this period:

(a) the employee remains on the payroll and retains insurance cover;
(b) leave balances are applied in the order SL, EL, CL, then Special Leave or Loss of Pay;
(c) the HRBP maintains fortnightly contact at a frequency agreed with the employee;
(d) a return-to-work plan, which may include phased hours or temporary role modification for
    up to eight (8) weeks, is agreed before the return date; and
(e) reasonable accommodation under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, is
    considered where the condition results in a long-term impairment.

## 12. Special Situations

### 12.1 Leave for Blood Donation

One (1) day of special paid leave per leave year on production of the donation certificate
from a licensed blood bank or a recognised donation camp.

### 12.2 Leave for Elections

Paid time off on the day of a general election, State assembly election or local body
election in the constituency in which the employee is registered, in accordance with Section
135B of the Representation of the People Act, 1951. Where the polling day is not a Company
holiday, employees are granted paid time off of not less than four (4) hours.

### 12.3 Leave for Jury or Statutory Duty

Paid time off for attendance at a court, tribunal or statutory authority in response to a
summons, on production of the summons. Where the employee is a party to litigation against
the Company, leave is applied against the Casual Leave or Earned Leave balance.

### 12.4 Leave for Sporting and Cultural Representation

Up to five (5) days of special paid leave per leave year for an employee selected to
represent the State or the country in a recognised sporting or cultural event, on production
of the selection letter from the recognised federation.

### 12.5 Quarantine and Public Health Directions

Where a public health authority directs an employee to isolate or quarantine, the period is
treated as paid special leave and is not deducted from any leave balance, on production of
the direction. Where the employee is able to work from home during the period, normal
attendance applies and no leave is deducted.

### 12.6 Leave for Employees with Disabilities

An employee with a benchmark disability as certified under the Rights of Persons with
Disabilities Act, 2016, is entitled to an additional four (4) days of paid leave per leave
year for medical review, therapy, assistive device fitting or related purposes. This
entitlement is in addition to Sick Leave and does not require exhaustion of any other
balance.

### 12.7 Gender Affirmation Leave

An employee undergoing gender affirmation treatment is entitled to up to thirty (30) days of
paid special leave in a rolling twenty-four-month period, on medical certification. The
application is handled directly by the CHRO's office, is not visible to the reporting
manager on PeopleHub, and is recorded as Special Leave without further description.

## 13. Manager Responsibilities

A reporting manager is accountable for:

1. Acting on every leave request within two (2) working days.
2. Maintaining a team leave calendar that is visible to the whole team.
3. Ensuring that no more than 25% of a team is on planned leave in the same week, and that
   critical-path roles have a named backup before leave begins.
4. Ensuring that every team member takes at least ten (10) days of Earned Leave in a leave
   year, including at least one block of five (5) continuous days.
5. Reviewing the monthly attendance exception report and closing exceptions within ten (10)
   working days.
6. Never asking a team member to work during approved leave. Contact during approved leave is
   permitted only for a Severity-1 incident and only through the formal recall procedure in
   Clause 5.5.
7. Raising Compensatory Off requests promptly for team members who work on a weekly off or a
   holiday at the manager's request.

Failure to discharge these responsibilities is assessed in the manager's own performance
review under the "People Leadership" goal.

## 15. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. My Casual Leave for the quarter is exhausted and I need a day off next week. What are
my options?**
Apply for Earned Leave if you have an accrued balance. If not, you may apply for Loss of Pay
with your manager's approval, or you may use a Compensatory Off if you hold one.

**Q2. I fell ill on a day I had applied for Casual Leave. Can I convert it?**
Yes. Raise a modification request on PeopleHub within seven days of return, attaching the
medical documentation if the absence exceeded two days.

**Q3. Do weekends count against my Earned Leave?**
Only for absences of more than five continuous calendar days. For an absence of five days or
fewer, intervening weekly offs and public holidays are not deducted.

**Q4. I earned a Compensatory Off in March and it is now June. Can I still use it?**
No. Compensatory Off must be availed within sixty days of the date earned, after which it
lapses. Your March credit expired in May.

**Q5. Can I encash my Casual Leave when I leave the Company?**
No. Only Earned Leave, up to a maximum of thirty days, is encashable on separation.

**Q6. I am a new father. How much leave do I get and when must I take it?**
Ten working days, in up to two blocks, taken within six months of the date of birth or the
date of handover of an adopted child.

**Q7. My manager has not acted on my leave request for four days. What happens?**
It escalates to your skip-level manager on day 3 and is auto-approved on day 5, unless it is
a sabbatical, special leave, or a request for more than fifteen days.

**Q8. I have 34 days of Earned Leave. What happens on 31 December?**
Thirty days carry forward. The remaining four days are encashed automatically in the January
payroll at (Basic + DA) ÷ 30 per day, and the amount is taxable.

**Q9. I am moving from Pune to Bengaluru for a six-month deputation. Whose holiday calendar
applies?**
Bengaluru's, because the deputation exceeds thirty continuous days.

**Q10. Independence Day 2026 falls on a Saturday. Do I get a compensatory day?**
No. A holiday falling on a weekly off is not compensated with an alternative day.
