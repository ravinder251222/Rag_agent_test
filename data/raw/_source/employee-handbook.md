## 1. About This Handbook

### 1.1 Purpose

This Handbook is the consolidated statement of the terms, conditions, expectations and
entitlements that govern employment at Nexora Technologies Private Limited ("Nexora", "the
Company"). It is issued under the authority of the Chief Human Resources Officer and is
binding on all persons who appear on the Nexora payroll.

The Handbook has three purposes. First, it tells you what the Company has committed to
provide you. Second, it tells you what the Company expects from you in return. Third, it
tells you where to go when something is unclear, unfair or has gone wrong. Where any of
these three things is not addressed here, the correct answer is not to improvise; it is to
raise a query with the HR Helpdesk at hrhelpdesk@nexoratech.in.

### 1.2 Scope and Applicability

This Handbook applies to:

(a) all confirmed employees of Nexora Technologies Private Limited across the Gurugram,
    Bengaluru and Pune offices;
(b) all employees serving probation, except where a clause is expressly stated to apply
    only on confirmation;
(c) all Graduate Engineer Trainees (GETs) enrolled in the Nexora Launchpad programme,
    except in respect of Clause 6.5 (Variable Pay), which does not apply during the
    training period; and
(d) all fixed-term contract employees on Nexora rolls, except in respect of Clause 7.4
    (Meal Card), Clause 7.6 (Relocation Assistance) and Clause 8.4 (Promotions).

This Handbook does **not** apply to independent contractors, consultants engaged on a
statement of work, vendor personnel, staffing-partner deputees or interns engaged for
fewer than 90 days. Those relationships are governed exclusively by their respective
contracts and by the Nexora Third Party Code of Conduct (LEG-POL-003).

The Company reserves the right to amend, withdraw or supplement any clause of this

Operations team and is audited annually as part of the ISO 27001:2022 surveillance audit.

### 2.1 Who We Are

Nexora Technologies Private Limited was incorporated in Haryana on 9 September 2011 under
CIN U72200HR2011PTC043117. We are a mid-sized IT services and engineering organisation of

build the careers of the people who write it.

- **Custody.** We treat client data, client systems and colleague trust as things held in
  custody, not things owned.

## 3. The Employment Relationship

### 3.1 Categories of Employment

**3.1.1 Permanent employees** are engaged without a fixed end date, subject to the notice
provisions in Clause 16.1. They are eligible for the full benefits set out in Clause 7.

**3.1.2 Probationers** are permanent employees who have not yet been confirmed. All new
hires, whether fresher or lateral, serve a probation period of six (6) months from the date
of joining. Probation may be extended once, by a maximum of three (3) months, by the
reporting manager with the written concurrence of the HR Business Partner and the
Band B6-or-above function head.

**3.1.3 Fixed-term contract employees** are engaged for a defined period, ordinarily
between six and twenty-four months, against a funded client engagement. Fixed-term
contracts do not renew automatically. Statutory benefits (Provident Fund, ESI where
applicable, gratuity where the statutory service condition is met) apply in full.

**3.1.4 Graduate Engineer Trainees (GETs)** are campus hires enrolled in Nexora Launchpad.
GETs serve a training period of eight (8) weeks followed by a probation of six (6) months
from the date of deployment to a project. GET appointments carry a service commitment of
eighteen (18) months from the date of joining, as recorded in the GET appointment letter.

**3.1.5 Interns** are engaged for a period of eight to twenty-four weeks against a defined
project. Interns receive a stipend, are not on the benefits programme, and are covered by
the Company's Group Personal Accident policy only.

### 3.2 Recruitment and Selection

Recruitment is conducted by the Talent Acquisition team under the Head of Talent
Acquisition. Every open role must have an approved Position Requisition on PeopleHub
carrying (i) the budget code, (ii) the band, (iii) the location, and (iv) the approval of
the function head and, for any position at Band B5 and above, of the Chief Financial
Officer.

Nexora is an equal opportunity employer. Selection is on merit alone. The Company does not
discriminate on the basis of religion, race, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual
orientation, place of birth, descent, marital status, pregnancy, disability, age or any
other status protected by law. Interview panels for Band B4 and above must include at
least one member from a different function than the hiring manager.

**Employee referrals.** Nexora encourages referrals. Referral awards are set out in
Clause 7.7. Employees must not participate in any stage of the selection process for a
candidate they have referred, nor for any relative as defined in Section 2(77) of the
Companies Act, 2013.

### 3.3 Offer, Documentation and Background Verification

An offer of employment is made in writing by the Talent Acquisition team and is valid for
seven (7) calendar days from the date of issue unless extended in writing. Verbal offers
are not binding on the Company.

Every offer is conditional upon successful completion of background verification (BGV),
which is conducted by our screening partner, VeriTrust Screening Services Private Limited.
The standard check set comprises:

| Check | Scope | Typical turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| Employment | Last 7 years or last 3 employers, whichever is shorter | 7–12 business days |
| Education | Highest qualification only | 10–21 business days |
| Address | Current permanent address, physical or digital | 5–7 business days |
| Criminal record | Court records at current and permanent address | 7–10 business days |
| Global database | Sanctions, PEP and adverse media screening | 2 business days |
| Drug screen | Only for engagements with clients contractually requiring it | 3 business days |

A candidate whose BGV returns a **Red** finding — a material discrepancy such as fabricated
employment, a forged qualification, or an undisclosed conviction — will have the offer
withdrawn, or, if already joined, will have their services terminated with immediate effect
under Clause 15.4. An **Amber** finding is reviewed by a panel comprising the HR Business
Partner, the hiring manager and a representative of Legal and Compliance.

Documents to be submitted on or before the date of joining are listed in HR-MAN-011,
Clause 2.3. The relieving letter and the final salary slip from the previous employer must
be uploaded within thirty (30) days of joining. Failure to do so places the confirmation
decision on hold and, after ninety (90) days, is treated as a Red BGV finding.

### 3.4 Probation and Confirmation

During probation, an employee's performance is reviewed formally at the end of month three
and month six by the reporting manager on the PeopleHub Probation Review form. The review
records observed performance against the role's 30-60-90 day plan (see HR-MAN-011,
Clause 6) and returns one of four outcomes:

| Outcome | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Confirm | Confirmation letter issued with effect from the completion date of probation |
| Extend | Probation extended by up to 3 months with a documented improvement plan |
| Redeploy | Employee moved to a different role or project; probation clock restarts at month 3 |
| Discontinue | Services terminated with 30 days' notice or notice pay in lieu |

Confirmation is not automatic. Where no confirmation decision is recorded within thirty
(30) days of the probation completion date, the employee is deemed confirmed with effect
from the probation completion date. During probation, the notice period on either side is
thirty (30) calendar days.

### 3.5 Internal Mobility

Employees who have completed eighteen (18) months in their current role and hold a
performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or better in the most recent cycle may apply for
internal openings through the PeopleHub Internal Job Board. The releasing manager may
withhold release for a maximum of sixty (60) days for engagement continuity; withholding
beyond sixty days requires the written approval of the Chief Operating Officer.

Internal transfers do not reset the probation clock, do not affect the date of joining for
gratuity or leave accrual purposes, and do not trigger a compensation revision except where
the move involves a band change, in which case Clause 8.4 applies.

### 3.6 Re-hire

Former employees who left in good standing, served their full notice, and completed exit
formalities are eligible for re-hire after a cooling period of six (6) months. Re-hire
requires the approval of the CHRO. A re-hired employee's earlier service does not count
towards gratuity, leave accrual or long-service recognition unless the break in service was
less than ninety (90) days and the re-hire was approved as a "continuity re-hire".

Employees who were terminated for cause, who abandoned service under Clause 4.7, or whose
BGV returned a Red finding are permanently ineligible for re-hire and are recorded as such
on the Do Not Rehire register maintained by HR Operations.

## 4. Working Hours, Attendance and Workplace Model

### 4.1 The Standard Work Week

The standard work week at Nexora is Monday to Friday. The standard working day is nine (9)
hours, inclusive of a sixty (60) minute unpaid meal break and two paid rest breaks of
fifteen (15) minutes each. This gives a net working time of eight (8) hours per day and
forty (40) hours per week.

Saturdays and Sundays are weekly offs. Where a client engagement requires a different weekly
pattern — for example a Sunday-to-Thursday week for a Gulf-region client — the pattern is
documented in the engagement's Delivery Operating Model and approved by the Delivery
Manager and HRBP. In no case will an employee be scheduled for more than six consecutive
working days without a weekly off.

### 4.2 Flexible Start and Core Hours

Nexora operates a flexible start window. Employees may begin work at any time between
08:00 and 11:00 IST, provided they complete their net eight hours and are available during
**core hours of 11:00 to 17:00 IST**. Core hours are when meetings, stand-ups, design
reviews and client calls are scheduled. Employees must not schedule recurring internal
meetings outside core hours without the agreement of every required attendee.

Flexibility is a facility, not an entitlement, and may be restricted at project level where
a client's contracted service window requires it. Any such restriction must be recorded in
the engagement's Delivery Operating Model.

### 4.3 Attendance Recording

Attendance is recorded in two ways, both of which are mandatory:

1. **Access-card swipe** at the turnstiles on entry to and exit from the office floor. The
   first swipe of the day and the last swipe of the day define the in-office duration.
2. **PeopleHub check-in**, completed from the Nexora network or through the PeopleHub
   mobile application with location services enabled.

A day is counted as follows:

| Net hours recorded | Attendance credit |
|---|---|
| 8.0 hours or more | Full day |
| 4.5 to 7.99 hours | Half day (0.5 day leave deducted) |
| Less than 4.5 hours | Absent (1 day leave deducted) |

Missed swipes must be regularised on PeopleHub within seven (7) calendar days through the
Attendance Regularisation workflow, with manager approval. A maximum of three (3)
regularisations per calendar month is permitted. Regularisation requests raised after the
seven-day window require HRBP approval and are granted only in exceptional circumstances.

### 4.4 Late Marks

An arrival recorded after 11:00 IST without a prior approved exception is a **late mark**.
Three (3) late marks in a calendar month result in the deduction of one half-day of Casual
Leave. Six (6) or more late marks in a calendar month are treated as an attendance
deficiency and are addressed under Clause 15.2 (Counselling).

Late marks are not recorded for employees on an approved shift roster, on client-site
deputation, on approved travel, or where the delay is attributable to a documented event
such as a transport strike or a civic emergency notified by the Facilities team.

### 4.5 Shifts

Nexora operates three shifts to support client service windows:

| Shift | Timing (IST) | Allowance per shift worked |
|---|---|---|
| General | 09:00–18:00 (flexi window applies) | Nil |
| Evening (Shift B) | 14:00–23:00 | INR 300 |
| Night (Shift C) | 21:30–06:30 | INR 550 |

Shift rosters are published by the Delivery Manager at least seven (7) calendar days before
the start of the roster month. No employee may be rostered on the night shift for more than
fourteen (14) consecutive calendar days, or for more than ninety (90) days in a rolling
twelve-month period, without their written consent recorded on PeopleHub.

In accordance with the Haryana Shops and Commercial Establishments rules and equivalent
State provisions in Karnataka and Maharashtra, women employees rostered between 20:00 and
06:00 are provided door-to-door Company-arranged transport with a security escort, and may
decline a night-shift roster without adverse consequence. The Facilities team maintains the
night transport roster and the escort attendance register at each site.

### 4.6 Hybrid Working

Nexora operates a hybrid working model. All employees, other than those designated
"Site-Anchored" or "Fully Remote", are required to work from their assigned office for a
minimum of **twelve (12) working days per calendar month**, of which at least one day each
month must coincide with the team's designated anchor day.

Designations are:

- **Site-Anchored** — roles requiring physical presence on all working days: IT Support,
  Facilities, Reception, Security, Lab and Hardware Engineering, and any role on a client
  engagement with a contractual on-site clause.
- **Hybrid** — the default designation for all other roles.
- **Fully Remote** — an exception approved by the function head and CHRO, reviewed every
  six months, and available only where the role has no on-site dependency and the employee
  has completed twelve (12) months of service.

Detailed provisions on eligibility, equipment, the internet reimbursement and cross-city
working are set out in the Remote and Hybrid Work Guidelines (HR-POL-012).

### 4.7 Absence Without Approval

Absence from work without an approved leave application or a communication to the reporting
manager is unauthorised absence and is treated as Loss of Pay for the days in question.

- **Three (3) consecutive working days** of unauthorised absence: the reporting manager
  contacts the employee on the registered mobile number and email, and records the attempt
  on PeopleHub.
- **Five (5) consecutive working days**: HR issues a written show-cause notice to the
  employee's registered address by email and registered post, requiring a response within
  seven (7) days.
- **Eight (8) consecutive working days** with no satisfactory response: the employee is
  deemed to have voluntarily abandoned service. The employment record is closed with effect
  from the first day of unauthorised absence, no relieving letter is issued, and the
  employee is placed on the Do Not Rehire register.

## 5. Leave — Summary

This clause is a summary only. The Leave and Attendance Policy (HR-POL-004) is the
governing document and prevails in the event of any inconsistency.

The Nexora leave year runs from **1 January to 31 December**, irrespective of the financial
year, which runs from 1 April to 31 March.

### 5.1 Leave Types and Annual Entitlement

| Leave type | Annual entitlement | Credit method | Carry forward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave (CL) | 12 days | 3 days credited on the first day of each quarter | Not permitted; lapses on 31 December |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 12 days | Credited in full on 1 January | Not permitted; lapses on 31 December |
| Earned Leave (EL) | 18 days | 1.5 days on completion of each calendar month | Up to 30 days |
| Compensatory Off | As earned | On approved work on a weekly off or holiday | Must be availed within 45 days of the date earned |
| Maternity Leave | 26 weeks | On application | Not applicable |
| Paternity Leave | 5 working days | On application | Not applicable |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 calendar days per event | On application | Not applicable |
| Marriage Leave | 5 working days, once in tenure | On application | Not applicable |

Leave in the year of joining is credited on a pro-rata basis from the date of joining.
Employees joining on or before the 15th of a month are credited for that full month;
employees joining on or after the 16th are credited from the following month.

### 5.2 Applying for Leave

All leave must be applied for on PeopleHub and approved by the reporting manager before it
is taken, except in the case of Sick Leave or a genuine emergency, which must be applied
for retrospectively on the first working day after return.

Advance notice requirements:

- Casual Leave: at least one (1) working day in advance.
- Earned Leave of three (3) or more consecutive days: at least seven (7) calendar days in
  advance.
- Earned Leave of five (5) or more consecutive days: at least fifteen (15) calendar days in
  advance.
- Any leave of ten (10) or more consecutive days: additionally requires the approval of the
  Delivery Manager and the HRBP.

Sick Leave of more than two (2) consecutive working days must be supported by a medical
certificate from a registered medical practitioner, uploaded to PeopleHub within seven (7)
days of return.

### 5.3 Public Holidays

Nexora observes ten (10) fixed holidays common to all locations, one or two location-specific
regional holidays, and two (2) optional holidays selected by the employee from a published
list. The holiday calendar for the following year is published by 30 November each year.

A fixed holiday that falls on a weekly off is not compensated with an alternative day.

### 5.4 Encashment

Earned Leave in excess of the thirty (30) day carry-forward cap is encashed automatically in
the January payroll at the rate of (Basic + Dearness Allowance) ÷ 30 per day. Casual Leave,
Sick Leave and Compensatory Off are not encashable at any time. On separation, accumulated
Earned Leave up to a maximum of thirty (30) days is encashed in the full and final
settlement.

## 6. Compensation — Summary

This clause is a summary only. The Compensation, Benefits and Payroll Policy (HR-POL-007) is
the governing document.

### 6.1 Salary Structure

Nexora expresses compensation as Cost to Company (CTC), which comprises Fixed Pay, Variable
Pay and Retirals. The standard fixed-pay construction is:

| Component | Basis |
|---|---|
| Basic Salary | 40% of annual Fixed Pay |
| House Rent Allowance | 50% of Basic Salary |
| Leave Travel Allowance | INR 40,000 per annum |
| Meal Card credit | INR 26,400 per annum (INR 2,200 per month) |
| Special Allowance | Balancing figure |

Retirals comprise the employer contribution to the Employees' Provident Fund, the gratuity
provision at 4.81% of Basic Salary, and, where opted, the employer contribution to the
National Pension System.

### 6.2 Payroll Cycle

The payroll month runs from the 1st to the last day of the calendar month. The attendance
and input cut-off is the **18th** of the month; changes received after the cut-off are
processed in the following month's payroll. Salary is credited on the **last working day**
of the month. Where the last working day falls on a bank holiday, credit is advanced to the
preceding working day.

Payslips are published on PeopleHub → My Pay by the 3rd working day of the following month.
Form 16 is issued by 15 June for the preceding financial year.

### 6.3 Annual Increment and Variable Pay

The annual compensation review is effective 1 July each year and is based on the performance
rating for the appraisal cycle ending 31 March. Variable pay is calculated on the annual
performance rating and the achievement of Company and business-unit targets, and is
disbursed with the June payroll. Variable pay opportunity by band is:

| Band | Variable pay as % of CTC |
|---|---|
| B1–B2 | 5% |
| B3 | 8% |
| B4 | 12% |
| B5–B6 | 15% |
| B7–B8 | 20% |

Employees who have resigned, or who are serving notice, on the date of disbursement are not
eligible for variable pay. Employees who joined on or after 1 January of the assessment year
receive variable pay on a pro-rata basis.

## 7. Benefits and Welfare

### 7.1 Group Insurance

| Policy | Cover | Who is covered | Employee cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group Mediclaim (GMC) | INR 5,00,000 family floater | Self, spouse, up to 2 dependent children | Nil |
| GMC — parental top-up | INR 5,00,000 floater for parents or parents-in-law | Optional, employee's election | INR 18,500 per parent per year, payroll-deducted |
| Group Personal Accident (GPA) | 3 × annual Fixed Pay, capped at INR 1,50,00,000 | Self | Nil |

The insurer is Sarvodaya General Insurance Company Limited and the third-party
administrator is MedAssist Services Private Limited. Enrolment of dependants must be

life event (marriage, childbirth, legal adoption). Outside those windows, additions are

Maternity is covered within the family floater up to INR 75,000 for a normal delivery and
INR 1,00,000 for a caesarean delivery, with a waiting period of nine (9) months from the
date of policy inception for the employee.

### 7.2 Statutory Retirals

**Provident Fund.** Both employee and employer contribute 12% of Basic Salary, subject to
the statutory wage ceiling of INR 15,000 per month for the employer's contribution unless
the employee has elected contribution on full Basic. Employees may make Voluntary Provident
Fund contributions of up to 88% of Basic; elections are accepted in April and October only.

**Gratuity.** Payable on separation after five (5) years of continuous service, computed as
(15 ÷ 26) × last drawn (Basic + DA) × completed years of service, subject to the statutory
cap. The five-year condition is waived in the case of death or permanent disablement.

**National Pension System.** Optional. Employees may direct up to 10% of Basic Salary as an
employer NPS contribution within their existing CTC, which is deductible under Section
80CCD(2) of the Income-tax Act, 1961. Elections are accepted twice a year, in April and
October.

### 7.3 Wellness and Employee Assistance

- **Annual health check.** A Company-funded annual health check at a partner diagnostic
  chain for all employees who have completed six months of service. Employees aged 40 and
  above receive the extended panel.
- **Employee Assistance Programme (EAP).** Nexora contracts with Ashray Wellbeing Services
  for confidential counselling. Employees and their immediate family members are entitled to
  eight (8) counselling sessions per person per year at no cost, accessible on 1800-102-8899
  or through the Ashray app. Nexora receives only anonymised utilisation statistics and
  never receives the identity of a caller or the content of a session.
- **Wellness allowance.** INR 6,000 per financial year, reimbursable against gym
  memberships, fitness classes, sports equipment, ergonomic accessories, or preventive
  health services. Claims are submitted on NexSpend under the "Wellness" category.
- **Creche support.** Reimbursement of up to INR 8,000 per month per child for children up
  to six years of age, in accordance with Section 11A of the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961,
  for a maximum of twenty-four (24) months per child.

### 7.4 Meal Card

All permanent employees receive a NexoCard meal card loaded with INR 2,200 per month. The
credit is part of Fixed Pay and is subject to the tax treatment applicable to meal vouchers.
Balances do not expire during employment but are forfeited thirty (30) days after the last
working day.

### 7.5 Salary Advance and Loans

Confirmed employees who have completed twelve (12) months of service may apply for a salary
advance of up to one (1) month's net salary, recoverable in six (6) equal monthly
instalments, once in any rolling twelve-month period. Applications are made on PeopleHub and
approved by the function head and the Head of Payroll.

An interest-free emergency medical advance of up to INR 2,00,000 is available for
hospitalisation of the employee or a dependant where the claim is not fully covered by

## 8. Performance Management

### 8.1 The Annual Cycle

The Nexora performance year runs from 1 April to 31 March. The cycle has four stages:

| Stage | Window | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Goal setting | 1–30 April | Employee, approved by manager |
| Mid-year check-in | 1–31 October | Manager |
| Self-assessment | 1–15 April (following year) | Employee |
| Manager assessment and normalisation | 16 April – 20 May | Manager, then the Normalisation Council |

Goals are recorded on PeopleHub → Performance and must be specific, measurable and
time-bound. Every employee carries a minimum of four and a maximum of seven goals, of which
at least one must be a learning goal aligned to the Individual Development Plan and one
must be a values goal.

### 8.2 Rating Scale

| Rating | Label | Indicative distribution |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding | 5–8% |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | 20–25% |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | 55–60% |
| 2 | Partially Meets Expectations | 8–12% |
| 1 | Below Expectations | 2–4% |

The indicative distribution is a guide for the Normalisation Council, not a quota to be
enforced within an individual team. A manager may propose a distribution outside the guide
with a written justification.

### 8.3 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

An employee rated 1 (Below Expectations), or whose performance materially declines during
the year, is placed on a Performance Improvement Plan of sixty (60) days, extendable once by
thirty (30) days. The PIP document must specify measurable outcomes, the support to be
provided, and the review dates. Fortnightly reviews are mandatory and are recorded on
PeopleHub. The HRBP attends the opening and closing reviews.

A successfully completed PIP is closed with a written confirmation. An unsuccessful PIP
leads to either redeployment or separation under Clause 15.4.

### 8.4 Promotions

Promotions are effective 1 July and are decided in the same cycle as the annual
compensation review. Eligibility requires:

(a) a minimum of eighteen (18) months in the current band;
(b) a rating of 4 or 5 in the most recent cycle, or two consecutive ratings of 3 with a
    documented readiness assessment;
(c) an open position at the target band in the approved manpower plan; and
(d) for Band B4 and above, clearance of a Promotion Panel comprising the function head, a
    Band B6-or-above leader from another function, and the HRBP.

Promotion to Band B5 and above additionally requires completion of the Nexora Leadership
Foundations programme (course code NEX-LDR-101).

## 10. Conduct, Ethics and Respectful Workplace — Summary

The Code of Conduct and POSH Policy (HR-POL-002) governs this subject in full. This clause
summarises the obligations that apply to every employee without exception.

1. **Integrity.** Do not offer, solicit or accept a bribe, kickback or facilitation payment.
   Gifts and hospitality above INR 5,000 in value must be declared on the PeopleHub Gift
   Register within seven days.
2. **Conflict of interest.** Declare any financial interest, directorship, family
   relationship or outside engagement that could reasonably be seen to conflict with your
   duties, on joining and annually thereafter.
3. **Moonlighting.** Employees must not take up concurrent employment, contract work or a
   directorship without the prior written approval of the CHRO. Passive investment,
   unpaid teaching, and open-source contribution outside client scope are permitted subject
   to Clause 11.
4. **Confidentiality.** Client data, Company data and colleague personal data must be used
   only for the purpose for which access was granted, and never removed from Company-managed
   systems.
5. **Respect.** Harassment, discrimination, bullying and retaliation are prohibited. Sexual
   harassment is dealt with under the statutory procedure set out in HR-POL-002, Part B.
6. **Substances.** Reporting for work under the influence of alcohol or a controlled
   substance is a serious misconduct. Nexora offices are alcohol-free except at events
   expressly authorised in writing by the site head.

### 10.1 Speaking Up

Any employee may raise a concern about suspected misconduct, fraud, a breach of law or a
breach of this Handbook through any of these channels, without fear of retaliation:

- the reporting manager or the skip-level manager;
- the HR Business Partner for the function;
- ethics@nexoratech.in, monitored by the General Counsel;
- the IntegriLine independent hotline on 1800-419-6272, available 24 hours in English,
  Hindi, Kannada, Marathi, Tamil and Telugu, with the option to remain anonymous.

Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith, or who participates in an
inquiry, is itself a serious misconduct and is dealt with under Clause 15.

## 15. Discipline

### 15.1 Standards

Nexora expects every employee to comply with this Handbook, the Code of Conduct, applicable
law and the reasonable and lawful instructions of their manager. Disciplinary action follows
a documented process and is proportionate to the conduct in question.

### 15.2 Progressive Action

| Level | Action | Typical trigger | Record retained |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verbal counselling | First instance of a minor lapse | Manager's note, not on personnel file |
| 2 | Written warning | Repeated minor lapse, or a first moderate lapse | Personnel file, 12 months |
| 3 | Final written warning | Repetition after a written warning | Personnel file, 24 months |
| 4 | Suspension pending inquiry | Alleged serious misconduct | Personnel file, permanent |
| 5 | Termination for cause | Established serious misconduct | Personnel file, permanent |

An employee at Level 3 or above is ineligible for promotion, variable pay above target, and
internal mobility for the duration of the record retention period.

### 15.3 Serious Misconduct

The following are, without limitation, serious misconduct and may lead to termination
without notice or notice pay:

(a) theft, fraud, forgery, falsification of records including timesheets and expense claims;
(b) wilful damage to Company or client property;
(c) unauthorised disclosure, removal or destruction of Company or client data;
(d) sexual harassment, physical violence, threats or intimidation;
(e) accepting or offering a bribe or kickback;
(f) reporting for work under the influence of alcohol or a controlled substance;
(g) conviction for an offence involving moral turpitude;
(h) concurrent employment in breach of Clause 10.3;
(i) deliberate misrepresentation in the employment application or during background
    verification;
(j) repeated wilful insubordination or refusal to carry out lawful instructions.

### 15.4 Inquiry Process

Where serious misconduct is alleged, the Company issues a written charge sheet setting out
the allegations and the evidence relied upon, and gives the employee not less than seven (7)
days to respond in writing. An Inquiry Officer, who has had no prior involvement in the
matter, conducts an inquiry at which the employee may be accompanied by a colleague of their
choice, may see the evidence, and may cross-examine witnesses.

The Inquiry Officer submits findings to the Disciplinary Authority (the function head for
Band B1–B4; the CHRO for Band B5 and above) within twenty-one (21) days of the conclusion of
the inquiry. The employee is informed of the decision in writing and may appeal to the
Appellate Authority (the COO) within fifteen (15) days.

An employee may be suspended pending inquiry on subsistence allowance of 50% of Basic Salary
for the first ninety (90) days and 75% thereafter.

## 16. Separation

### 16.1 Resignation and Notice

An employee wishing to resign must submit the resignation on PeopleHub → Separation. The
notice period for all confirmed employees is **ninety (90) calendar days**. During probation
the notice period is thirty (30) calendar days on either side.

The notice period may be waived or shortened only with the written approval of the function
head and the CHRO. Where a shortfall is approved, the employee pays notice-shortfall
recovery calculated on (Basic + Dearness Allowance) ÷ 30 per day of shortfall. Earned Leave
may not be adjusted against the notice period, and leave taken during notice extends the
last working day by an equivalent number of days unless expressly waived by the function
head.

A resignation may be withdrawn only with the written consent of the function head and the
CHRO, and only before the last working day.

### 16.2 Exit Process

| Step | Owner | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Resignation acknowledged; last working day confirmed | HR Operations | Within 3 working days |
| Knowledge transfer plan agreed and signed | Employee and manager | Within 10 working days |
| Exit interview | HRBP | In the last 10 working days |
| Asset return and NexServe clearance | Employee, IT | Last working day |
| Finance, library, facilities and access-card clearance | Employee | Last working day |
| Relieving letter and experience letter issued | HR Operations | Within 5 working days of the last working day |
| Full and final settlement credited | Payroll | Within 45 days of the last working day |

### 16.3 Full and Final Settlement

The settlement includes salary for days worked, encashment of Earned Leave up to thirty (30)
days, the gratuity amount where payable, and any variable pay expressly declared before the
resignation date. It is reduced by notice-shortfall recovery, outstanding advances,
unreturned assets at depreciated value, recoverable relocation or education assistance, and
statutory deductions.

### 16.4 Termination by the Company

The Company may terminate employment by giving the same notice as required from the
employee, or salary in lieu, other than in cases of termination for cause under Clause 15.3,
where no notice is required. Termination on grounds of redundancy is preceded by a
redeployment search of not less than thirty (30) days and, where applicable, by compliance
with the notice and compensation requirements of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.

### 16.5 Retirement

The age of superannuation is fifty-eight (58) years, calculated to the last day of the month
in which the employee attains that age. Re-engagement on a fixed-term consultancy after
superannuation is possible with the approval of the CEO.

### 16.6 Post-Employment Obligations

Confidentiality obligations survive the end of employment indefinitely in respect of client
data and trade secrets. The non-solicitation obligation in the appointment letter — not to
solicit Nexora employees or clients — survives for twelve (12) months. Employees must not
represent themselves as associated with Nexora after the last working day and must update
public professional profiles within fifteen (15) days.

### 16.7 Alumni

Nexora maintains an alumni network for employees who leave in good standing. Alumni receive
the quarterly newsletter, invitations to the annual technology conference, and priority
consideration for re-hire under Clause 3.6.

## 17. Definitions

**Anchor day** — the one working day each week on which a team is expected to be co-located
in the office, published by the team's manager.

**Band** — the career level assigned to a role, from B1 to B8, as set out in Clause 2.4.

**Basic Salary** — the component of Fixed Pay from which Provident Fund, gratuity, leave
encashment and notice recovery are computed.

**Confirmed employee** — a permanent employee who has successfully completed probation and
holds a confirmation letter, or who is deemed confirmed under Clause 3.4.

**Continuous service** — uninterrupted service with the Company, treating approved leave,
statutory maternity leave and Company-directed deputation as service.

**Cost to Company (CTC)** — the aggregate annual cost of employment, comprising Fixed Pay,
Variable Pay and Retirals.

**Date of joining (DOJ)** — the date on which the employee first reported for duty, as
recorded on PeopleHub. Leave accrual, gratuity and long-service recognition run from this
date.

**Delivery Operating Model (DOM)** — the engagement-level document that records working
hours, shift pattern, on-site requirements and escalation contacts for a client engagement.

**Fixed Pay** — the guaranteed annual cash component of CTC, excluding Variable Pay and
Retirals.

**Full and final settlement (F&F)** — the closing account between the Company and a
separating employee, as described in Clause 16.3.

**Loss of Pay (LOP)** — a day of absence for which no leave balance is available and for
which salary is not paid.

**Net working hours** — recorded in-office or logged-in duration excluding the meal break.

**PeopleHub** — the Nexora human resources management system of record.

**NexServe** — the Nexora IT service management and asset management platform.

**NexSpend** — the Nexora travel, expense and reimbursement platform.

**NexoraLearn** — the Nexora learning management system.

**Weekly off** — Saturday and Sunday, unless a different pattern is recorded in the
engagement's Delivery Operating Model.
