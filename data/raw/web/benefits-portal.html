Everything Nexora provides beyond your salary, in one place. Enrolment, claims and nominations are all done on PeopleHub; this page explains what you are entitled to and how to use it.

Policy year 1 July 2026 – 30 June 2027. Additions to the medical policy outside a 30-day joining or life-event window can only be made at renewal. Diarise 1 June if you plan to add a dependant.

The bits people miss

  • Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one. There is no waiting period on the base policy.
  • Room rent is capped at 1.5% of the sum insured per day — that is INR 7,500 a night on a INR 5,00,000 floater. ICU is at actuals. If you take a room above the cap, the insurer proportions every line on the bill down, not just the room charge. This is the single largest cause of unexpected out-of-pocket cost.
  • Maternity is INR 75,000 normal / INR 1,00,000 caesarean, with a nine-month waiting period from the date your cover started.
  • A newborn is covered from day one, but you must tell MedAssist within 30 days of the birth.
  • Mental health is covered on the same terms as physical illness, including in-patient psychiatric care.
  • There is a 15% co-payment on parental top-up claims. There is none on claims for you, your spouse or your children.

4. Support

Employee Assistance Programme

Ashray Wellbeing Services, 1800-102-8899, 24 hours. Eight counselling sessions per person per year, free, for you and each immediate family member. Confidential — Nexora receives anonymised utilisation numbers only, never names and never session content. Available from your first day, including during probation.

Annual health check

Company-funded after six months of service. Book through the Benefits tile on PeopleHub. Colleagues aged 40 and over get the extended panel, which adds cardiac screening and, where clinically indicated, a stress test.

Advances

Salary advance of up to one month's net pay, recovered over six months, once in a rolling year, after twelve months of service. Separately, an interest-free emergency medical advance of up to INR 2,00,000 is available for hospitalisation not fully covered by insurance — CHRO and CFO approve jointly, and in practice this is turned around in two working days.

Nominations. A surprising number of colleagues have never completed PF Form 2, Gratuity Form F or the Group Term Life nomination. Without a nomination on file, a claim by your family goes through succession formalities and can take months. Check PeopleHub → My Profile → Nominations today.
