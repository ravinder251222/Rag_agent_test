Travel and Expense Reimbursement

Owner: Accounts Payable, Finance & Accounts  ·  Last updated: 2026-01-19  ·  Last reviewed: 19 January 2026  ·  Classification: Internal Use Only
travelexpensesnexspendper diemadvance

How to get travel approved, how to book it, and how to be reimbursed without a three-week argument with Finance. Governed by FIN-POL-006.

The two rules that cause 90% of rejected claims. (1) Approval before travel, recorded on NexSpend — not a Slack message, not a verbal yes. (2) Claims within 30 days of the trip ending.

2. Booking

Book through Trailhead Corporate Travel — the desk is reachable at travel@nexoratech.in or on the NexSpend "Book travel" tile. Self-booked travel is reimbursed at the lower of what you paid and what the desk could have got, and only where you had written approval to self-book first. In practice, self-booking an expensive last-minute fare is the most common way people end up out of pocket.

4. Claiming

  1. Open NexSpend → New Claim and link it to the approved travel request. A claim with no linked request goes to Finance for manual review and adds about a week.
  2. Attach an original tax invoice for every line above INR 500. A card statement is not an invoice. A screenshot of a booking confirmation is not an invoice.
  3. Enter the GSTIN where the vendor issued a GST invoice to Nexora — the Company cannot claim input credit without it, and Finance will send the claim back.
  4. Submit within 30 days of the trip ending. Between 30 and 60 days needs your function head's approval. Beyond 60 days needs the CFO, and is normally refused.

5. Advances

A travel advance of up to 80% of the estimated cost is available for trips over INR 25,000 or any international trip. Settle it within 15 days of returning. Unsettled advances are recovered from payroll without further notice, and you cannot hold two open advances at once.

6. What is not reimbursed

  • Alcohol, other than at an event approved in advance by the site head.
  • In-flight wifi, seat selection and priority boarding, unless the flight is over six hours.
  • Fines, penalties, traffic challans and parking tickets.
  • Personal entertainment, minibar, laundry on trips under four nights, and spa or gym charges.
  • Travel insurance bought separately — Nexora already covers you on approved travel.
  • Costs for a family member accompanying you, including the difference on a shared room.
  • Anything paid in cash above INR 10,000 in a single transaction — disallowed under section 40A(3) of the Income-tax Act, 1961.
Expense fraud. Claiming for something you did not spend, altering an invoice, or splitting a claim to duck an approval threshold is serious misconduct under the Code of Conduct, clause 2.7. Finance samples 8% of claims for substantive testing every month and every claim above INR 50,000.
