Owner: Priyanka Bhattacharya, Director - Talent Acquisition  ·  Last updated: 2026-05-04  ·  Last reviewed: 4 May 2026  ·  Classification: Internal Use Only

Roles open to internal applicants. Applying does not need your manager's permission and your manager is not notified until you reach the second stage.

Who can apply

  • 18 months completed in your current role on the date you apply.
  • A rating of "Meets Expectations" or better in the most recent cycle.
  • Not on a live written warning and not on a Performance Improvement Plan.

If you are within three months of the 18-month mark and the role is a strong fit, apply anyway and note it in your application — the HRBP can waive the tenure requirement once.

How it works

StageWhat happensTimeline
1. ApplySubmit on PeopleHub. Your manager is not told.—
2. ScreenTA partner reviews against the requisition5 working days
3. ConversationInformal chat with the hiring managerWithin 10 working days
4. PanelFormal interview; your manager is now informedWithin 15 working days
5. Decision and releaseRelease negotiated between managersRelease within 60 days; longer needs COO approval

An internal move does not restart your probation, does not change your date of joining for gratuity or leave, and does not by itself change your compensation unless the move involves a band change.

Questions

Will my manager find out that I applied?

Not until stage 4. The screen and the first conversation with the hiring manager are confidential.

Can my manager block my move?

They can hold your release for up to 60 days for engagement continuity, and they have to say what specific delivery commitment is at risk. Anything beyond 60 days needs the COO to sign off.

Do I get a pay rise?

Only if the move is to a higher band, in which case the promotion increment guidelines apply. A lateral move at the same band does not change your compensation.

I was not selected. Can I apply for something else?

Yes, immediately. There is no cooling-off period for internal applications, and an unsuccessful application is not recorded against you.
