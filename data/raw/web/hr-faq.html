HR Frequently Asked Questions

Owner: HR Communications  ·  Last updated: 2024-11-08  ·  Last reviewed: 8 November 2024  ·  Classification: Internal Use Only

Heads up. This FAQ is a plain-English summary maintained by the HR Communications team. It is not the policy. The controlled documents live on PeopleHub → Policies. If something here contradicts a policy document, the policy document wins.

Leave

How much leave do I actually get in a year?

Forty-two days of paid leave, plus public holidays. It splits like this:

TypeDaysHow you get itDoes it carry over?
Casual Leave (CL)123 days land on 1 Jan, 1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 OctNo — use it or lose it on 31 Dec
Sick Leave (SL)12All 12 on 1 JanuaryNo
Earned Leave (EL)181.5 days for every full month you workYes, up to 30 days

The leave year is the calendar year (January to December). This trips people up because the appraisal year and the financial year both run April to March.

Can I take Casual Leave for a week?

No. Casual Leave maxes out at three consecutive days. Anything longer has to be Earned Leave, and you need to give notice — seven days for a three-day break, fifteen days for five days or more.

I worked a Saturday. How long do I have to use the comp-off?

45 days from the date you earned it. After that it lapses and it is not encashable. Your manager has to raise the comp-off request on PeopleHub — it is not automatic just because you badged in on a weekend.

My partner is having a baby. What leave can I take?

Paternity Leave is 5 working days, taken within six months of the birth. Upload the birth certificate on PeopleHub. If you are the birthing parent, Maternity Leave is 26 weeks for your first two children.

What happens to Earned Leave I do not use?

Up to 30 days rolls into next year. Anything above 30 days on 31 December is encashed automatically in the January payroll at (Basic + DA) ÷ 30 per day, and it is taxable. You do not need to apply for this.

Do weekends get deducted if I take a long holiday?

Only if the absence runs longer than five continuous calendar days. For a short break, the weekend in the middle is free.

Attendance and working from the office

How many days do I have to be in the office?

If you are on the Hybrid designation, 12 working days a month, including your team's anchor day. Site-Anchored roles (IT Support, Facilities, Reception, Security, lab and hardware work, and anyone on a client engagement with an on-site clause) come in every working day. Fully Remote is an exception that needs your function head and the CHRO to sign off, and it is reviewed every six months.

What counts as a full day?
Net hours loggedWhat it counts as
8 hours or moreFull day
4.5 to 7.99 hoursHalf day — half a day of leave comes off
Under 4.5 hoursAbsent — a full day of leave comes off

Net hours exclude the one-hour meal break. You need both an access-card swipe and a PeopleHub check-in; one without the other shows up as an exception on your manager's dashboard.

I keep getting in after 11. Does it matter?

Yes. After 11:00 IST without a prior exception is a late mark. Three in a month costs you half a day of Casual Leave. More than eight in a month is treated as an attendance deficiency and your manager has to counsel you formally.

I forgot to swipe. Can I fix it?

Regularise it on PeopleHub within seven calendar days. You get three regularisations a month without needing HRBP approval.

Pay and payroll

When does salary hit my account?

The last working day of the month. If that is a bank holiday it moves earlier, never later. Payslips appear on PeopleHub → My Pay by the third working day of the following month.

I changed my bank account on the 22nd and salary went to the old one.

The payroll input cut-off is the 18th. Anything after that lands in the next month's run. There is no off-cycle payment for this.

Why is my take-home so much less than my CTC ÷ 12?

Because CTC includes variable pay, the employer PF contribution and the gratuity provision, none of which are paid to you monthly, and because PF, professional tax and income tax come off the monthly figure. The worked example in the Compensation, Benefits and Payroll Policy walks through it line by line.

Leaving Nexora

What is my notice period?

90 days once you are confirmed, whatever your band. During probation it is 30 days on either side. Your function head and the CHRO can agree to shorten it, in which case you pay notice-shortfall recovery on (Basic + DA) ÷ 30 per day.

Can I use up my Earned Leave instead of serving notice?

No. Leave cannot be set against the notice period, and any leave you do take during notice pushes your last working day out by the same number of days unless your function head waives it in writing.

When do I get my full and final settlement?

Within 45 days of your last working day, provided asset return and clearance are done. Form 16 for that year comes by 15 June, sent to the personal email address on your PeopleHub profile — so make sure that is current before you leave.

Benefits

Who is covered on the medical policy?

You, your spouse and up to two dependent children, on a INR 5,00,000 family floater. Parents or parents-in-law can be added on the top-up at your cost. Enrolment windows are 30 days from joining, 30 days from a life event (marriage, childbirth, adoption), or the 1 July renewal.

Can I talk to someone confidentially?

Yes. Ashray Wellbeing Services runs our Employee Assistance Programme — 1800-102-8899, eight free sessions a year for you and each immediate family member. Nexora only ever sees anonymised usage numbers, never who called or what was said.

Still stuck?

Raise a ticket on PeopleHub → Helpdesk or write to hrhelpdesk@nexoratech.in. First response within one business day, resolution within three. For anything about conduct, ethics or harassment, use the channels on the Speak Up page instead — those do not go through the HR helpdesk queue.
