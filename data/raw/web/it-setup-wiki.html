IT Setup Wiki for New Joiners

Owner: Deepak Bhatia, Director - IT  ·  Last updated: 2026-02-26  ·  Last reviewed: 26 February 2026  ·  Classification: Internal Use Only
onboardinglaptopvpnaccessacceptable use

Written for people in their first fortnight. Everything here assumes you have already collected your laptop from the IT desk on day one. If you have not, raise a P2 on NexServe and tell your manager — do not wait.

Day 1 — before you do anything else

  1. Sign in and enrol MFA. Your account is firstname.lastname@nexoratech.in. First sign-in forces a password change and MFA enrolment. Use the Microsoft Authenticator app; SMS fallback is available but is being retired in the second half of 2026.
  2. Check the device is enrolled. Open NexServe → My Assets and confirm your serial number is listed against your name. Disk encryption, endpoint protection and the DLP agent are pre-installed. If any shows "Non-compliant", raise a P2 — a non-compliant device is blocked from client environments.
  3. Connect to the VPN. Nexora Secure Access. The profile is pushed automatically; if it is missing, install it from Company Portal.
  4. Set up Slack. You are added to #general, #site-gurugram or your site channel, and your team channel. Set your display name to your full name, not a handle.

The IT desk is at Level 3 in Gurugram, Level 6 in Bengaluru and Level 3 in Pune, staffed 09:00–19:00 IST. Outside those hours a P1 pages the on-call engineer.

Software

Install from the NexServe software catalogue. Anything not in the catalogue needs a request with a business justification and, for anything that touches client data, a security review. Do not install unlicensed software, and do not use a personal licence for work — both are breaches of the Acceptable Use Policy.

Generative AI — the short version

  • Use the enterprise instances provisioned by Nexora. Personal accounts, personal API keys and unapproved browser extensions are not permitted for work.
  • No client-confidential material, no personal data, no credentials, no proprietary source code goes into any service that is not on the approved list.
  • Some clients prohibit AI-assisted development outright. The engagement's Delivery Operating Model records the position for your client — check it before you start, not after.
  • Anything a model generates, you own. Review it, test it, licence-scan it. The reviewer is accountable for the code as if they had written it themselves.

Things that will get your access suspended

  • Plugging a personal USB drive into a Nexora laptop.
  • Forwarding work email, code or documents to a personal address.
  • Sharing your password, MFA token or access card. Even once. Even with your own manager.
  • Turning off the VPN on an untrusted network.
  • Not completing your mandatory courses. At day 45 everything except PeopleHub and NexoraLearn is suspended until you finish them.

Lost or stolen device

Report it to NexServe and security-incident@nexoratech.in within 24 hours. If it went missing outside Company premises, file a police FIR and send a copy. Reporting quickly is what protects you; the Company can recover the depreciated value up to INR 40,000 only where the loss followed demonstrable negligence, and prompt reporting weighs against that finding.

Working from home

You need a stable connection of at least 20 Mbps down and 5 Mbps up, a quiet place for client calls, and a power arrangement that survives an outage. The INR 1,500 monthly internet and telephone allowance is paid with salary against a declaration — you do not submit bills for it. A UPS or inverter for your router is claimable once every three years under the wellness and workplace category, up to INR 8,000.
