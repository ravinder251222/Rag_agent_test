[S1] hr-faq.html
How much leave do I actually get in a year?
Forty-two days of paid leave, plus public holidays. It splits like this:

Type Days How you get it Does it carry over?
Casual Leave (CL) 12 3 days land on 1 Jan, 1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct No — use it or lose it on 31 Dec
Sick Leave (SL) 12 All 12 on 1 January No
Earned Leave (EL) 18 1.5 days for every full month you work Yes, up to 30 days

The leave year is the calendar year (January to December). This trips people
up because the appraisal year and the financial year both run April to March.

Can I take Casual Leave for a week?
No. Casual Leave maxes out at three consecutive days. Anything longer has to
be Earned Leave, and you need to give notice — seven days for a three-day break,
fifteen days for five days or more.

[S2] leave-and-attendance-policy.pdf, page 8
3. Casual Leave (CL)
3.1 Purpose and Quantum
Casual Leave covers short, unplanned personal absences: a domestic emergency, a civic appointment, a
school event, a vehicle breakdown, a religious observance not covered by the holiday calendar. The
entitlement is twelve (12) days per leave year, credited three (3) days on the first day of each quarter.
3.2 Rules
1. Casual Leave may be taken in units of half a day or one full day.
2. Not more than three (3) consecutive days of Casual Leave may be taken at one time.
An absence longer than three days must be applied as Earned Leave.
3. Casual Leave may not be combined with Earned Leave in a single continuous absence, except
where the Casual Leave is the first or last day of the absence and the total absence does not exceed five
(5) days.

[S4] hr-glossary.md
**Buddy** — A colleague assigned to a new joiner for their first 30 days for practical
and social integration. Not a mentor, not a manager, and explicitly not the person who
handles performance, leave or grievances. INR 3,000 on completion.

**Carry forward** — Unused Earned Leave rolled into the next leave year, capped at 30
days. Anything above the cap is auto-encashed in the January payroll. Casual and Sick
Leave never carry forward.

**Casual Leave (CL)** — 12 days a year, credited 3 days per quarter, maximum 3
consecutive days at a time, lapses on 31 December, never encashable.

**Compa-ratio** — Your CTC divided by the midpoint of your band's salary range. Below
0.85 or above 1.15 needs written justification from the function head at every review.
